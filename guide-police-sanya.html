Guide to Obtaining a Certificate of No Criminal Record in Sanya, China

Learn how foreigners can obtain a Sanya police clearance certificate, including visa eligibility, required documents, application procedures and notarization.

By: China Attest

Last Updated: 26 November 2026

1. Overview

Foreign nationals who have lived in Sanya may need to obtain a Certificate of No Criminal Record for various purposes, including immigration, employment, school attendance, and other overseas applications.

The certificate is also commonly referred to as a police certificate, police check, or police clearance certificate in other countries.

For overseas use, a Certificate of No Criminal Record issued by Chinese public security authorities generally needs to be notarized by a local notary office in China. Depending on the destination country, the notarized document may also require an apostille issued by China's Ministry of Foreign Affairs or consular legalization by the relevant embassy.

This guide explains the eligibility considerations, required documents, application procedures, and notarization process for foreign nationals seeking a Sanya Certificate of No Criminal Record.

2. Eligibility

Based on practical application experience, foreign nationals holding residence permits issued in Sanya have generally been able to obtain Certificates of No Criminal Record successfully.

However, it is unclear whether foreign nationals holding other types of visas are also eligible.

These visa types may include:

  • M Visa: Trade and commerce.
  • F Visa: Exchange and visit activities.
  • L Visa: Tourism.

Applicants holding these or other visa types should confirm their eligibility with the Sanya Public Security Bureau before submitting an application.

3. Required Documents

Step 1: Application for a Certificate of No Criminal Record

The following documents are required:

  • Current Passport: The applicant's current passport.
  • Previous Passport(s): If the applicant used an old passport during the period covered by the requested criminal record check, both the old and current passports must be provided.
  • Power of Attorney: Required if a third party is appointed to handle the application on the applicant's behalf.

Applicants should prepare all relevant passports covering the period for which the certificate is requested.

Step 2: Notarization of the Certificate of No Criminal Record

The following documents are required:

  • Passport(s).
  • Certificate of No Criminal Record: The certificate issued by the Sanya Public Security Bureau.
  • Power of Attorney: Required if a third party is appointed to handle the notarization application, where applicable.
  • Temporary Residence Registration Form: Some notary officers may also request this document.

Applicants should confirm the latest document requirements with their selected notary office before submission.

4. Application Process

Obtaining a Sanya Certificate of No Criminal Record for overseas use generally involves two main steps.

Step 1: Apply for a Certificate of No Criminal Record

Foreign nationals can submit their applications to the Division of Exit and Entry Administration of the Sanya Public Security Bureau.

Applicants can check the contact information of the relevant office through China's National Immigration Administration (NIA) website by selecting Sanya.

Applicants should prepare their current passports, previous passports if applicable, and a Power of Attorney if a third party is appointed.

Processing Time: Approximately 4 working days.

Step 2: Obtain Notarization of the Certificate

After receiving the Certificate of No Criminal Record from the Sanya Public Security Bureau, applicants can submit it to a local notary office for the issuance of a Notarial Certificate of No Criminal Record.

Currently, two notary offices in Sanya are licensed to handle foreign-related notarization:

  1. Sanya Notary Public Office.
  2. Sanya Fenghuang Notary Public Office.

Applicants can consult either office to confirm its current foreign-related notarization services and document requirements.

Processing Time: Approximately 1–3 working days.

Additional Authentication for Overseas Use

Depending on the destination country, the notarized document may also need:

  • Apostille: Issued by China's Ministry of Foreign Affairs or an authorized office.
  • Consular Legalization: Authentication by the relevant embassy when applicable.

The required authentication procedure depends on the destination country's document requirements.

5. Application Authority

Certificate of No Criminal Record

Applications can be submitted to:

English Name: Division of Exit and Entry Administration, Sanya Public Security Bureau.

Chinese Name: 三亚市公安局出入境管理部门.

Applicants can visit the National Immigration Administration (NIA) official website and select Sanya to find the relevant office's contact information.

Applicants should confirm the current application arrangements before visiting.

Notarization

According to the information provided for this guide, two notary offices in Sanya are licensed to handle foreign-related notarization.

Available Options:

  1. Sanya Notary Public Office.
  2. Sanya Fenghuang Notary Public Office.

Applicants can consult either office to confirm current services, contact details, required documents, and processing arrangements.

6. Special Requirements

Eligibility for Different Visa Types

Based on practical application experience, foreign nationals holding residence permits issued in Sanya have generally been able to obtain Certificates of No Criminal Record successfully.

However, the eligibility of applicants holding other visa types has not been clearly established in the information available for this guide.

Applicants holding M (trade and commerce), F (exchange and visit), L (tourism), or other visa types should confirm their eligibility with the relevant Sanya public security authority.

Previous Passports

Applicants who used previous passports during the period covered by their criminal record check must provide both their previous and current passports.

This helps establish the applicant's identity and passport history during the relevant period.

Power of Attorney for Third-Party Applications

If another person is appointed to handle the application, a Power of Attorney is required.

Applicants should prepare the authorization document before submitting the application and confirm any additional requirements with the relevant authority.

Temporary Residence Registration Forms

Some notary officers in Sanya may request a Temporary Residence Registration Form when processing the notarization application.

Although this document is not listed as a standard mandatory requirement in the original application information, applicants are advised to prepare it if available.

Sample Notarial Certificate of No Criminal Record

According to the original information provided for this guide, notary offices across China issue Notarial Certificates of No Criminal Record in a consistent format.

Notarization for Overseas Use

A Certificate of No Criminal Record issued by local Chinese police is generally not sufficient for overseas immigration, employment, or educational applications without notarization.

Applicants should submit the certificate to a local notary office licensed to handle foreign-related notarization.

Additional Authentication for Overseas Use

Depending on the destination country, the notarized document may also require an apostille or consular legalization.

Applicants should confirm the requirements with the receiving authority before proceeding.

7. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Where can foreigners apply for a Sanya Certificate of No Criminal Record?

Applications can be submitted to the Division of Exit and Entry Administration of the Sanya Public Security Bureau.

Applicants can find its contact information through the National Immigration Administration website by selecting Sanya.

What are the eligibility requirements?

Based on practical application experience, foreign nationals holding residence permits issued in Sanya have generally been able to obtain Certificates of No Criminal Record successfully.

The eligibility of applicants holding other types of visas is unclear and should be confirmed with the local public security authority.

Can foreigners holding an M, F, or L visa apply?

The eligibility of foreign nationals holding M (trade and commerce), F (exchange and visit), or L (tourism) visas is not clearly established in the information available for this guide.

Applicants should confirm their eligibility with the Sanya Public Security Bureau before applying.

Do I need to provide my previous passport?

Yes. If you used an old passport during the period covered by the requested certificate, you must provide both your old and current passports.

How long does the application take?

The Certificate of No Criminal Record application generally takes approximately 4 working days.

Notarization generally takes an additional 1–3 working days.

Where can I notarize my Sanya Certificate of No Criminal Record?

According to the information provided for this guide, two notary offices in Sanya are licensed to handle foreign-related notarization:

  • Sanya Notary Public Office.
  • Sanya Fenghuang Notary Public Office.

Do I need a Temporary Residence Registration Form for notarization?

Some notary officers may request this document. Applicants are advised to prepare it if available and confirm the requirements with their selected notary office.

Does the notarized certificate need an apostille?

This depends on the destination country. Some countries require an apostille, while others may require consular legalization.

Applicants should confirm the requirements with the receiving authority before proceeding.
