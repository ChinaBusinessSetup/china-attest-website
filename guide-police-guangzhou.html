Guide to Obtaining a Certificate of No Criminal Record in Guangzhou, China

Learn how foreigners can obtain a Guangzhou police clearance certificate, including eligibility, residence registration, required documents, application and notarization.

By: China Attest

Last Updated: 26 November 2026

1. Overview

Foreign nationals who have lived in Guangzhou may need to obtain a Certificate of No Criminal Record for various purposes, including immigration, employment, and other overseas applications.

The certificate is also commonly referred to as a police certificate, police check, or police clearance certificate in other countries.

The issuance of Certificates of No Criminal Record in Guangdong Province is governed by two important regulations:

  • The Provisions of Guangdong Provincial Public Security Department Regarding the Application for and the Handling of Certificates of No Criminal Record, which came into effect on March 1, 2019.
  • The Provisions on the Handling of Criminal Record Checks by Public Security Organs, issued by China's Ministry of Public Security in 2022.

According to the original information provided for this guide, both regulations are currently effective.

Foreign nationals may need to obtain a Certificate of No Criminal Record in China for various purposes, including immigration, employment, and other overseas applications.

For overseas use, the certificate generally needs to be notarized by a local notary office in China. Depending on the destination country, the notarized document may also require an apostille issued by China's Ministry of Foreign Affairs or consular legalization by the relevant embassy.

This guide explains the eligibility requirements, required documents, application procedures, and notarization process for foreign nationals seeking a Guangzhou Certificate of No Criminal Record.

2. Eligibility

Foreign nationals applying for a Certificate of No Criminal Record in Guangzhou must meet the following requirements:

  • Must have had residence permits issued in Guangzhou, as evidenced by their Temporary Residence Registration Form(s).
  • Must have lived in Guangzhou for 180 days or longer.

Applicants should prepare their Temporary Residence Registration Forms and other relevant supporting documents to demonstrate their residence history in Guangzhou.

3. Required Documents

Step 1: Application for a Certificate of No Criminal Record

The following documents are required:

  • Passport(s): Relevant passport documents covering the applicant's residence history in Guangzhou.
  • Temporary Residence Registration Form(s): Documents evidencing the applicant's residence registration in Guangzhou.
  • Power of Attorney: Required if a third party is appointed to handle the application on the applicant's behalf.

Step 2: Notarization of the Certificate of No Criminal Record

The following documents are required:

  • Certificate of No Criminal Record: The certificate issued by the Guangzhou Public Security Bureau.
  • Passport(s).
  • Power of Attorney: Required if another person is appointed to submit the notarization application.
  • Photograph of the Applicant Holding the Signed Power of Attorney: Required when another person is appointed to handle the notarization application, according to the requirements stated in this guide.

Applicants should confirm the latest document requirements with the selected notary office before submission.

4. Application Process

Obtaining a Guangzhou Certificate of No Criminal Record for overseas use generally involves two main steps.

Step 1: Apply for a Certificate of No Criminal Record

Foreign nationals may submit their applications to the Division of Exit and Entry Administration of the Guangzhou Public Security Bureau.

The application office is:

Division of Exit and Entry Administration, Guangzhou Public Security Bureau

Address: No. 155 Jiefang South Road, Yuexiu District, Guangzhou, China.

Chinese Address: 广州市越秀区解放南路155号

Telephone: +86 020-12367

Office Hours:

  • Monday to Friday: 8:30 AM–12:00 PM and 2:00 PM–5:30 PM.
  • Closed on public holidays.

Applicants should prepare their passports, Temporary Residence Registration Forms, and a Power of Attorney if a third party is appointed.

Processing Time: Approximately 3 business days in most cases.

Step 2: Obtain Notarization of the Certificate

After receiving the Certificate of No Criminal Record from the Guangzhou Public Security Bureau, applicants can submit it to a local notary office for the issuance of a Notarial Certificate of No Criminal Record.

There are multiple notary offices in Guangzhou that provide notarization services for documents intended for overseas use.

Guangzhou Notary Public Office is one of the experienced options for foreign-related notarization.

Applicants should confirm the specific requirements and appointment arrangements with their selected notary office before submission.

Processing Time: Approximately 1–2 weeks.

Additional Authentication for Overseas Use

Depending on the destination country, the notarized document may also need:

  • Apostille: Issued by China's Ministry of Foreign Affairs or an authorized office.
  • Consular Legalization: Authentication by the relevant embassy when applicable.

The required authentication procedure depends on the destination country's document requirements.

6. Special Requirements

Temporary Residence Registration Forms

Foreign nationals applying for a Guangzhou Certificate of No Criminal Record should provide their Temporary Residence Registration Forms as evidence of their residence registration in Guangzhou.

Applicants should prepare the relevant forms covering their residence history.

Power of Attorney for Third-Party Applications

If another person is appointed to submit the application for a Certificate of No Criminal Record, a Power of Attorney is required.

For the notarization application, the requirements stated in this guide also include a photograph of the applicant holding the signed Power of Attorney.

Applicants should confirm the current requirements with the relevant authority or notary office before proceeding.

Sample Notarial Certificate of No Criminal Record

Notarization for Overseas Use

A Certificate of No Criminal Record is generally not sufficient for overseas immigration, employment, or educational applications without notarization.

Applicants should check the document requirements of their destination country to determine whether an apostille or consular legalization is also necessary.

7. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Where can foreigners apply for a Guangzhou Certificate of No Criminal Record?

Applications can be submitted to the Division of Exit and Entry Administration of the Guangzhou Public Security Bureau at No. 155 Jiefang South Road, Yuexiu District, Guangzhou.

What are the eligibility requirements?

Foreign nationals must have had residence permits issued in Guangzhou, evidenced by their Temporary Residence Registration Forms, and must have lived in Guangzhou for at least 180 days.

What documents are required for the application?

Applicants generally need their passports, Temporary Residence Registration Forms, and a Power of Attorney if a third party is appointed to handle the application.

How long does the application take?

The application generally takes approximately 3 business days in most cases.

Notarization generally takes an additional 1–2 weeks.

Where can I notarize my Guangzhou Certificate of No Criminal Record?

There are multiple notary offices in Guangzhou that provide foreign-related notarization services. Guangzhou Notary Public Office is one of the experienced options.

What documents are needed for notarization?

Applicants generally need their passports and the Certificate of No Criminal Record.

If another person is appointed to submit the notarization application, a Power of Attorney and a photograph of the applicant holding the signed Power of Attorney are also required according to the information in this guide.

Does the notarized certificate need an apostille?

This depends on the destination country. Some countries require an apostille, while others may require consular legalization.

Applicants should confirm the requirements with the receiving authority before proceeding.
