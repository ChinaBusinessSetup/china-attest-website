Guide to Obtaining a Certificate of No Criminal Record in Suzhou, China

Learn how foreigners can obtain a Suzhou police clearance certificate, including eligibility, required documents, district application offices, notarization and apostille.

By: China Attest

Last Updated: 26 November 2026

1. Overview

Foreign nationals who have lived in Suzhou may need to obtain a Certificate of No Criminal Record for various purposes, including immigration, employment, and other overseas applications.

The certificate is also commonly referred to as a police certificate, police check, or police clearance certificate in other countries.

The procedures for obtaining a Certificate of No Criminal Record in Suzhou were updated in March 2022 following the issuance of the Provisions on the Handling of Criminal Record Checks by Public Security Organs, which came into effect on December 31, 2021.

For overseas use, the certificate generally needs to be notarized by a local notary office in China. Depending on the destination country, the notarized document may also require an apostille issued by China's Ministry of Foreign Affairs or consular legalization by the relevant embassy.

This guide explains the eligibility requirements, required documents, application procedures, and notarization process for foreign nationals seeking a Suzhou Certificate of No Criminal Record.

2. Eligibility

Foreign nationals applying for a Certificate of No Criminal Record in Suzhou must meet the following requirements:

  • Must have held residence permits issued in Suzhou.
  • Must have lived in Suzhou for 180 days or longer.

Applicants should prepare relevant documents to demonstrate their residence history and registration in Suzhou.

3. Required Documents

Step 1: Application for a Certificate of No Criminal Record

The following documents are required:

  • Original Passport(s): Applicants must provide their original passport documents.
  • Power of Attorney: Required if a third party is appointed to handle the application on the applicant's behalf.

Applicants should prepare the relevant passport documents covering their residence history in Suzhou.

Step 2: Notarization of the Certificate of No Criminal Record

The following documents are required:

  • Passport(s).
  • Certificate of No Criminal Record: The certificate issued by the relevant Suzhou public security authority.
  • Power of Attorney: Required if a third party is appointed to handle the notarization application, where applicable.

Applicants should confirm the latest document requirements with their selected notary office before submission.

4. Application Process

Obtaining a Suzhou Certificate of No Criminal Record for overseas use generally involves two main steps.

Step 1: Apply for a Certificate of No Criminal Record

Foreign nationals must submit their applications to the district branch office of the Division of Exit and Entry Administration of the Suzhou Public Security Bureau that has jurisdiction over the district where they have lived and registered their residence.

Applicants should apply through the relevant district office rather than an unrelated district office.

The National Immigration Administration (NIA) provides contact information for Exit and Entry Administration offices throughout China. Applicants can visit its official website and select Suzhou to find the relevant district office.

Applicants should prepare their original passports and a Power of Attorney if a third party is appointed.

Processing Time: Approximately 3 working days.

Step 2: Obtain Notarization of the Certificate

After receiving the Certificate of No Criminal Record from the Suzhou Public Security Bureau, applicants can submit it to a local notary office licensed to notarize Certificates of No Criminal Record for overseas use.

There are multiple notary offices in Suzhou providing foreign-related notarization services.

Recommended Option: Suzhou Notary Public Office.

Applicants can find contact information for notary offices throughout Suzhou through the Jiangsu Notary Organizations and Notary Officers Credit Information Check Platform by selecting Suzhou.

Processing Time: Approximately 1–2 weeks.

Additional Authentication for Overseas Use

Depending on the destination country, the notarized document may also need:

  • Apostille: Issued by China's Ministry of Foreign Affairs or an authorized office.
  • Consular Legalization: Authentication by the relevant embassy when applicable.

The required authentication procedure depends on the destination country's document requirements.

5. Application Authority

Certificate of No Criminal Record

Applications must be submitted to the district branch office of the Division of Exit and Entry Administration of the Suzhou Public Security Bureau that has jurisdiction over the district where the applicant has lived and registered their residence.

Application Authority: District branch offices of the Division of Exit and Entry Administration, Suzhou Public Security Bureau.

Applicants can visit the National Immigration Administration (NIA) official website and select Suzhou to find contact information for the relevant district offices.

Applicants should confirm the appropriate district office and its current application arrangements before visiting.

Notarization

There are multiple notary offices in Suzhou licensed to handle foreign-related notarization of Certificates of No Criminal Record for overseas use.

Recommended Option: Suzhou Notary Public Office.

Applicants can also consult the Jiangsu Notary Organizations and Notary Officers Credit Information Check Platform to find contact information for other notary offices in Suzhou.

Applicants may choose a suitable notary office according to their location and service requirements.

6. Special Requirements

Applying at the Correct District Office

An important requirement in Suzhou is that the application must be submitted to the district branch office that has jurisdiction over the area where the applicant has lived and registered their residence.

Applicants should identify their relevant residence registration district before submitting an application.

Original Passport Requirement

According to the information provided for this guide, applicants must provide their original passports when applying for a Certificate of No Criminal Record in Suzhou.

Applicants should prepare the relevant original passport documents before visiting the public security authority.

Power of Attorney for Third-Party Applications

If another person is appointed to handle the application, a Power of Attorney is required.

Applicants should prepare the authorization document before submitting the application and confirm any additional requirements with the relevant authority or notary office.

Notarization for Overseas Use

A Certificate of No Criminal Record is generally not sufficient for overseas immigration, employment, or educational applications without notarization.

Applicants should submit the certificate to a local notary office licensed to handle foreign-related notarization.

Sample Notarial Certificate of No Criminal Record

Notary offices across China issue Notarial Certificates of No Criminal Record in a consistent format, according to the information provided for this guide.

Additional Authentication for Overseas Use

Depending on the destination country, the notarized document may also require an apostille or consular legalization.

Applicants should confirm the requirements with the receiving authority before proceeding.

7. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Where can foreigners apply for a Suzhou Certificate of No Criminal Record?

Applications must be submitted to the district branch office of the Division of Exit and Entry Administration of the Suzhou Public Security Bureau that has jurisdiction over the district where the applicant has lived and registered their residence.

What are the eligibility requirements?

Foreign nationals must have held residence permits issued in Suzhou and must have lived in Suzhou for at least 180 days.

Which district office should I visit?

You should apply at the district branch office that has jurisdiction over the area where your residence is registered.

Applicants can check the relevant office information through the National Immigration Administration website by selecting Suzhou.

Do I need to provide my original passport?

Yes. According to the information provided for this guide, the original passport is required for the application.

What documents are required for the application?

Applicants generally need their original passports and a Power of Attorney if a third party is appointed to handle the application.

How long does the application take?

The Certificate of No Criminal Record application generally takes approximately 3 working days.

Notarization generally takes an additional 1–2 weeks.

Where can I notarize my Suzhou Certificate of No Criminal Record?

Applicants can choose from multiple notary offices in Suzhou licensed to handle foreign-related notarization.

Suzhou Notary Public Office is one of the options mentioned in this guide.

Does the notarized certificate need an apostille?

This depends on the destination country. Some countries require an apostille, while others may require consular legalization.

Applicants should confirm the requirements with the receiving authority before proceeding.
