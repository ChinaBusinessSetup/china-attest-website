Guide to Obtaining a Certificate of No Criminal Record in Wuhan, China

Learn how foreigners can obtain a Wuhan police clearance certificate, including eligibility, required documents, district application offices, notarization and apostille.

By: China Attest

Last Updated: 26 November 2026

1. Overview

Foreign nationals who have lived in Wuhan may need to obtain a Certificate of No Criminal Record for various purposes, including immigration, employment, and other overseas applications.

The certificate is also commonly referred to as a police certificate, police check, or police clearance certificate in other countries.

For overseas use, a Certificate of No Criminal Record issued by Chinese public security authorities generally needs to be notarized by a local notary office in China. Depending on the destination country, the notarized document may also require an apostille issued by China's Ministry of Foreign Affairs or consular legalization by the relevant embassy.

This guide explains the eligibility requirements, required documents, application procedures, and notarization process for foreign nationals seeking a Wuhan Certificate of No Criminal Record.

2. Eligibility

Foreign nationals applying for a Certificate of No Criminal Record in Wuhan must meet the following requirements:

  • Must have held residence permits issued in Wuhan.
  • Must have lived in Wuhan for 180 days or longer.

Applicants should prepare relevant documents to demonstrate their residence history and registration in Wuhan.

3. Required Documents

Step 1: Application for a Certificate of No Criminal Record

The following documents are required:

  • Passport(s): Relevant passport documents covering the applicant's residence history in Wuhan.
  • Power of Attorney: Required if a third party is appointed to handle the application on the applicant's behalf.

Step 2: Notarization of the Certificate of No Criminal Record

The following documents are required:

  • Passport(s).
  • Certificate of No Criminal Record: The certificate issued by the relevant Wuhan public security authority.
  • Power of Attorney: Required if a third party is appointed to handle the notarization application, where applicable.

Applicants should confirm the latest document requirements with their selected notary office before submission.

4. Application Process

Obtaining a Wuhan Certificate of No Criminal Record for overseas use generally involves two main steps.

Step 1: Apply for a Certificate of No Criminal Record

Foreign nationals must submit their applications to the district branch office of the Division of Exit and Entry Administration of the Wuhan Public Security Bureau that has jurisdiction over the district where they have lived and registered their residence.

The relevant district branch office is determined by the applicant's residence registration details, which can be found in their Temporary Residence Registration Form.

Applicants can check the contact information of the Exit and Entry Administration branch offices in Wuhan through China's National Immigration Administration (NIA) website by selecting Wuhan.

Applicants should prepare their passports and a Power of Attorney if a third party is appointed.

Processing Time: Approximately 3 working days.

Step 2: Obtain Notarization of the Certificate

After receiving the Certificate of No Criminal Record from the Wuhan Public Security Bureau, applicants can submit it to a local notary office licensed to issue Notarial Certificates of No Criminal Record for overseas use.

There are multiple notary offices in Wuhan that provide foreign-related notarization services.

Applicants can find contact information for notary offices through the official websites of:

  • Wuhan Justice Bureau.
  • Wuhan Notary Public Officers Association.

Processing Time: Approximately 3–5 working days.

Additional Authentication for Overseas Use

Depending on the destination country, the notarized document may also need:

  • Apostille: Issued by China's Ministry of Foreign Affairs or an authorized office.
  • Consular Legalization: Authentication by the relevant embassy when applicable.

The required authentication procedure depends on the destination country's document requirements.

5. Application Authority

Certificate of No Criminal Record

Applications must be submitted to the district branch office of the Division of Exit and Entry Administration of the Wuhan Public Security Bureau that has jurisdiction over the district where the applicant has lived and registered their residence.

Application Authority: District branch offices of the Division of Exit and Entry Administration, Wuhan Public Security Bureau.

The appropriate district office is determined by the applicant's residence registration details, which can be found in their Temporary Residence Registration Form.

Applicants can visit the National Immigration Administration (NIA) official website and select Wuhan to find contact information for the relevant district offices.

Applicants should confirm the appropriate district office and its current application arrangements before visiting.

Notarization

There are multiple notary offices in Wuhan licensed to issue Notarial Certificates of No Criminal Record for overseas use.

Applicants can find information about these notary offices through:

  1. Wuhan Justice Bureau.
  2. Wuhan Notary Public Officers Association.

Applicants may choose a suitable notary office according to their location and service requirements.

6. Special Requirements

Applying at the Correct District Office

An important requirement in Wuhan is that the application must be submitted to the district branch office that has jurisdiction over the area where the applicant has lived and registered their residence.

Applicants should check their Temporary Residence Registration Forms to identify the relevant district before submitting their applications.

Residence Permits Issued in Wuhan

Foreign nationals applying for a Wuhan Certificate of No Criminal Record must have held residence permits issued in Wuhan and lived there for at least 180 days.

Applicants should prepare relevant documents to demonstrate their residence history and eligibility.

Power of Attorney for Third-Party Applications

If another person is appointed to handle the application, a Power of Attorney is required.

Applicants should prepare the authorization document before submitting the application and confirm any additional requirements with the relevant public security authority or notary office.

Notarization for Overseas Use

A Certificate of No Criminal Record is generally not sufficient for overseas immigration, employment, or educational applications without notarization.

Applicants should submit the certificate to a local notary office licensed to handle foreign-related notarization.

Sample Notarial Certificate of No Criminal Record

Notary offices across China issue Notarial Certificates of No Criminal Record in a consistent format, according to the information provided for this guide.

Additional Authentication for Overseas Use

Depending on the destination country, the notarized document may also require an apostille or consular legalization.

Applicants should confirm the requirements with the receiving authority before proceeding.

7. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Where can foreigners apply for a Wuhan Certificate of No Criminal Record?

Applications must be submitted to the district branch office of the Division of Exit and Entry Administration of the Wuhan Public Security Bureau that has jurisdiction over the district where the applicant has lived and registered their residence.

What are the eligibility requirements?

Foreign nationals must have held residence permits issued in Wuhan and must have lived in Wuhan for at least 180 days.

Which district office should I visit?

You should apply at the district branch office that has jurisdiction over the area where your residence is registered.

Your Temporary Residence Registration Form can help identify the relevant district.

What documents are required for the application?

Applicants generally need their passports and a Power of Attorney if a third party is appointed to handle the application.

How long does the application take?

The Certificate of No Criminal Record application generally takes approximately 3 working days.

Notarization generally takes an additional 3–5 working days.

Where can I notarize my Wuhan Certificate of No Criminal Record?

Applicants can choose from multiple notary offices in Wuhan licensed to issue Notarial Certificates of No Criminal Record for overseas use.

Information about these offices is available through the Wuhan Justice Bureau and Wuhan Notary Public Officers Association.

Can someone apply on my behalf?

Yes. A third party may be appointed to handle the application using a Power of Attorney.

Applicants should confirm the applicable authorization requirements with the relevant public security authority and notary office.

Does the notarized certificate need an apostille?

This depends on the destination country. Some countries require an apostille, while others may require consular legalization.

Applicants should confirm the requirements with the receiving authority before proceeding.

The following authorities and resources are relevant to the Wuhan application and notarization procedures:

  1. National Immigration Administration of China (NIA) – Contact information for district Exit and Entry Administration offices throughout China, including Wuhan.
  2. Wuhan Justice Bureau – Information about notary offices and notarization services in Wuhan.
  3. Wuhan Notary Public Officers Association – Information about notary offices licensed to provide notarization services in Wuhan.
  4. Provisions on the Handling of Criminal Record Checks by Public Security Organs – General regulations governing criminal record checks in China.

Applicants should consult the relevant Wuhan public security authorities and notary offices to confirm the latest application requirements, district jurisdiction, and document requirements before submitting their applications.
