Guide to Obtaining a Certificate of No Criminal Record in Shenzhen, China

Learn how to obtain a Shenzhen police clearance certificate, including eligibility, required documents, application procedures, notarization and apostille.

by: China Attest

Last Updated: 25 November 2026

1. Overview

Foreign nationals who have lived in Shenzhen may need to obtain a Certificate of No Criminal Record for various purposes, including immigration, employment, and other overseas applications.

The certificate is also commonly referred to as a police certificate, police check, or police clearance certificate in other countries.

Shenzhen has a dedicated administrative history for handling applications for Certificates of No Criminal Record issued to foreign nationals.

On March 13, 2014, the Office of No Criminal Record Certificate of the Shenzhen Public Security Bureau (PSB) was officially established. In 2018, the Shenzhen Administration Service Hall replaced this office as the government agency responsible for accepting applications.

On July 10, 2023, the application submission location changed again to the Exit and Entry Administration of the Shenzhen Municipal Public Security Bureau. The Office of No Criminal Record Certificate remains the issuing authority, according to the information provided for this guide.

Foreign nationals may need to obtain a Certificate of No Criminal Record in China for various purposes, including immigration, employment, and other overseas applications.

For overseas use, the certificate generally needs to be notarized by a local notary office in China. Depending on the destination country, the notarized document may also require an apostille issued by China's Ministry of Foreign Affairs or consular legalization by the relevant embassy.

This guide explains the eligibility requirements, required documents, application procedures, and notarization process for foreign nationals seeking a Shenzhen Certificate of No Criminal Record.

2. Eligibility

Foreign nationals applying for a Certificate of No Criminal Record in Shenzhen must meet the following requirements:

  • Must have held residence permits issued in Shenzhen.
  • Must have lived in Shenzhen for 180 days or longer.

Applicants should prepare supporting documents demonstrating their residence history and eligibility.

3. Required Documents

Step 1: Application for a Certificate of No Criminal Record

The following documents are required:

  • Original Passport: The original passport must be provided, or a passport copy that has been either apostilled by the competent foreign affairs authority in the issuing country or authenticated by a Chinese embassy or consulate.
  • Copies of Previous Passports: Copies of any relevant previous passports covering the applicant's residence history in Shenzhen.
  • Power of Attorney: Required if a third party is appointed to handle the application on the applicant's behalf.

If the applicant is living outside China, the Power of Attorney must be either:

  • Apostilled by the competent foreign affairs authority in the applicant's country; or
  • Authenticated by a Chinese embassy or consulate in that country.

Step 2: Notarization of the Certificate of No Criminal Record

The following documents are required:

  • Passport(s).
  • Certificate of No Criminal Record: The certificate issued by the Shenzhen Public Security Bureau.
  • Power of Attorney: Required if a third party is appointed to handle the notarization application, where applicable.

The specific document requirements should be confirmed with the selected notary office before submission.

4. Application Process

Obtaining a Shenzhen Certificate of No Criminal Record for overseas use generally involves two main steps.

Step 1: Apply for a Certificate of No Criminal Record

Foreign nationals who meet the eligibility requirements must submit their applications to the Exit and Entry Administration of the Shenzhen Municipal Public Security Bureau.

The application filing location is:

Exit and Entry Administration of Shenzhen Municipal Public Security Bureau

Applicants should prepare their original passports, relevant previous passport copies, and a properly authenticated Power of Attorney if a third party is appointed.

Processing Time: Approximately 3 -5 working days.

Step 2: Obtain Notarization of the Certificate

After receiving the Certificate of No Criminal Record from the Shenzhen Public Security Bureau, applicants can submit it to a local notary office for the issuance of a Notarial Certificate of No Criminal Record with an English translation.

For overseas purposes such as immigration to the United States, Canada, Australia, and other countries, the notarized certificate may be required in addition to the original police certificate.

Shenzhen has multiple notary offices providing foreign-related notarization services.

One major option is the Shenzhen Notary Public Office. Applicants may also choose another notary office according to their location and convenience.

Processing Time: Approximately 1–2 weeks.

Additional Authentication for Overseas Use

Depending on the destination country, the notarized document may also need:

  • Apostille: Issued by China's Ministry of Foreign Affairs or an authorized office.
  • Consular Legalization: Authentication by the relevant embassy when applicable.

The required authentication procedure depends on the destination country's document requirements.

6. Special Requirements

Power of Attorney for Applicants Outside China

If an applicant is living outside China and appoints a third party to handle the application, the Power of Attorney must be properly authenticated.

It must either be apostilled by the competent foreign affairs authority in the applicant's country or authenticated by a Chinese embassy or consulate in that country, as applicable.

Passport Authentication

For the application, the original passport must be provided, or a passport copy that has been apostilled by the competent foreign affairs authority or authenticated by a Chinese embassy or consulate, according to the requirements stated in this guide.

Applicants should confirm the accepted passport documentation with the Shenzhen Exit and Entry Administration before submitting their applications.

Sample Notarial Certificate of No Criminal Record

All notary offices across China issue Notarial Certificates of No Criminal Record in the same format. Please take a look at our article, China Police Certificate Samples: What the Certificate and Notarial Document Look Like

Notarization for Overseas Use

A Certificate of No Criminal Record is generally not sufficient for overseas immigration, employment, or educational applications without notarization.

Applicants should check the document requirements of their destination country to determine whether an apostille or consular legalization is also necessary.

7. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Where can foreigners apply for a Shenzhen Certificate of No Criminal Record?

Applications must be submitted to the Exit and Entry Administration of the Shenzhen Municipal Public Security Bureau.

The application address provided in this guide is No. 4016 Jiefang Road, Luohu District, Shenzhen Government Affairs Service Center.

What are the eligibility requirements?

Foreign nationals must have held residence permits issued in Shenzhen and must have lived in Shenzhen for at least 180 days.

How long does the application take?

The Certificate of No Criminal Record application generally takes approximately 3 -5 working days.

Notarization generally takes an additional 1–2 weeks.

What documents are required?

Applicants generally need their original passports or properly authenticated passport copies, copies of relevant previous passports, and a Power of Attorney if a third party is appointed.

The notarization stage requires the passport(s), the Certificate of No Criminal Record, and a Power of Attorney where applicable.

Can someone apply on my behalf?

Yes. A third party may be appointed to handle the application using a Power of Attorney.

If the applicant is living outside China, the Power of Attorney must be apostilled or authenticated by a Chinese embassy or consulate, as applicable.

Where can I notarize my Shenzhen Certificate of No Criminal Record?

Applicants can submit their certificates to a local notary office in Shenzhen that handles foreign-related notarization.

Shenzhen Notary Public Office is one available option. There are also other notary offices in the city.

Does the notarized certificate need an apostille?

This depends on the destination country. Some countries require an apostille, while others may require consular legalization.

Applicants should confirm the requirements with the receiving authority before proceeding.
