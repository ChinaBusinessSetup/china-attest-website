01CSSD / CHSI Verification

Chinese University Qualifications

Need to verify a qualification awarded by a Chinese university? China Attest can help you understand the CSSD/CHSI report requirements for your application, including applications to credential evaluation organizations such as WES. Before applying, confirm which reports your receiving organization requires for your qualification, degree or academic transcript.

02CSCSE Qualification Recognition

Overseas Qualifications for Use in China

If you earned your qualification outside China and need to use it in China, your receiving organization may request recognition through the Chinese Service Center for Scholarly Exchange (CSCSE). Contact China Attest to discuss your qualification, its intended use and whether assistance is available for your case.

Enquire About CSCSE Recognition
03Apostille & Consular Authentication

Chinese Academic Documents for Use Abroad

Using a Chinese diploma, degree certificate or academic transcript overseas? Depending on the destination and the receiving organization’s requirements, you may need notarization followed by an apostille or consular authentication. These procedures serve a different purpose from academic verification, and some applications may require both. China Attest can help you clarify the required steps.

Enquire About Document Authentication
04Foreign-Issued Documents

Overseas Academic Documents for Use in China

Academic documents issued outside China may need an apostille or consular authentication before they can be used in China. The required procedure depends on the issuing country and the receiving organization’s requirements. Some steps must be completed in the country of issue. Before sending any originals, contact China Attest to confirm the requirements and the assistance available.
