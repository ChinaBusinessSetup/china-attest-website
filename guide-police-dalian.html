Guide to Obtaining a Certificate of No Criminal Record in Dalian, China

Learn how foreigners can obtain a Dalian police clearance certificate, including the notary introduction letter, eligibility, district police application and final notarization.

By: China Attest

Last Updated: 26 November 2026

1. Overview

Foreign nationals who have lived in Dalian may need to obtain a Certificate of No Criminal Record for various purposes, including immigration, employment, and other overseas applications.

The certificate is also commonly referred to as a police certificate, police check, or police clearance certificate in other countries.

In August 2021, an important change took place in the procedures for obtaining Certificates of No Criminal Record in Dalian.

Previously, foreign nationals could apply directly to Dalian Notary Public Office for a Notarial Certificate of No Criminal Record. Since August 2021, the procedure has generally involved multiple visits between the notary office and the local public security authorities.

For overseas use, a Certificate of No Criminal Record issued by local Chinese public security authorities generally needs to be notarized by a local notary office in China. Depending on the destination country, the notarized document may also require an apostille issued by China's Ministry of Foreign Affairs or consular legalization by the relevant embassy.

The Dalian procedure generally involves three steps:

  1. Obtain a Notary Introduction Letter from Dalian Notary Public Office.
  2. Apply for a Certificate of No Criminal Record at the relevant district Exit and Entry Administration office.
  3. Return to the notary office for final notarization and translation.

Please note that the Introduction Letter may no longer be required by some police offices. Applicants should contact the relevant district office to confirm the current requirements before visiting the notary office.

This guide explains the eligibility requirements, required documents, application procedures, and notarization process for foreign nationals seeking a Dalian Certificate of No Criminal Record.

2. Eligibility

There are no publicly available written rules specifically setting out the eligibility requirements for foreign nationals applying for a Certificate of No Criminal Record in Dalian.

Based on practical application experience, foreign nationals generally need to meet the following conditions:

  • Must have held residence permits issued in Dalian.
  • Must have had Temporary Residence Registration Forms issued by local police stations in Dalian.
  • Must have lived in Dalian for six months or longer.

These requirements are based on practical application experience rather than publicly available written eligibility rules. Applicants should confirm their individual eligibility with the relevant district Exit and Entry Administration office before applying.

3. Required Documents

The Dalian application procedure involves three stages, each with its own document requirements.

Step 1: Obtaining a Notary Introduction Letter

The following documents are required:

  • Passport(s): The applicant's passport documents.
  • Power of Attorney: Required if a third party is appointed to handle the application, where applicable.

Step 2: Application for a Certificate of No Criminal Record

The following documents are required:

  • Notary Introduction Letter: The Introduction Letter issued by Dalian Notary Public Office, if required by the relevant police office.
  • Passport(s).
  • Power of Attorney: Required if a third party is appointed to handle the application.
  • Application Form: The application form required by the relevant district Exit and Entry Administration office.

Step 3: Final Notarization

The main document required is:

  • Certificate of No Criminal Record: The certificate issued by the relevant local public security authority in Dalian.

Applicants should return to the notary office and submit the police-issued certificate for final notarization.

Applicants should confirm any additional document requirements with the notary office before submission.

4. Application Process

Obtaining a Dalian Certificate of No Criminal Record for overseas use generally involves three main steps.

Step 1: Obtain a Notary Introduction Letter

The first step is to visit Dalian Notary Public Office and request an Introduction Letter for submission to the local public security authority.

The letter is intended to support the subsequent application for a Certificate of No Criminal Record.

According to the information provided for this guide, the letter may be required by the relevant district Exit and Entry Administration office. However, some police offices may no longer require it.

Applicants should contact the relevant district police office to confirm whether the Introduction Letter is currently necessary before arranging this step.

Notary Office: Dalian Notary Public Office.

Address: Refer to the Dalian Notary Public Office location information.

Phone: +86 0411 65850147.

Processing Time: Usually issued on the spot.

Step 2: Apply for a Certificate of No Criminal Record

After obtaining the Notary Introduction Letter, if required, applicants can submit their applications to the Exit and Entry Administration office of the Dalian Public Security Bureau that has jurisdiction over the district where they lived in Dalian.

Dalian has multiple district-level Exit and Entry Administration service offices.

Applicants must apply at the office responsible for their area of residence.

The available information for this guide includes a list of district service windows. Applicants should consult the relevant official contact information to identify the correct office before visiting.

Processing Time: Approximately 3–5 working days.

Step 3: Return to the Notary Office for Final Notarization

After receiving the Certificate of No Criminal Record from the local public security authority, applicants must return to the notary office and submit the certificate for final notarization.

The notary office will issue a Notarial Certificate of No Criminal Record with an English translation or another language translation as requested by the applicant.

Please note that most notary offices will keep the original Certificate of No Criminal Record on file when processing the notarization application.

This means applicants generally will no longer possess the original police-issued certificate after submitting it for notarization.

Processing Time: Approximately 3–5 working days.

Additional Authentication for Overseas Use

Depending on the destination country, the notarized document may also need:

  • Apostille: Issued by China's Ministry of Foreign Affairs or an authorized office.
  • Consular Legalization: Authentication by the relevant embassy when applicable.

The required authentication procedure depends on the destination country's document requirements.

Certificate of No Criminal Record

6. Special Requirements

Notary Introduction Letter

An important feature of the Dalian application procedure is the Notary Introduction Letter.

The applicant may need to obtain this letter from Dalian Notary Public Office before applying for a Certificate of No Criminal Record through the local public security authority.

However, according to the information provided for this guide, some district police offices may no longer require the letter.

Applicants should confirm whether the Introduction Letter is currently necessary with the relevant district office before proceeding.

Background of the Notary Introduction Letter

The Introduction Letter procedure is related to an older regulation issued in 1990 titled Notice Regarding the Handling of Certificates of Whether Foreigners Have Criminal Sanctions During the Period of Their Residence in China.

This historical regulation gave notaries the authority to issue Notarial Certificates of No Criminal Record to foreign nationals and to investigate relevant criminal record information with local police authorities.

Although the original procedures are no longer implemented in every detail, they may help explain the historical background of the notary's involvement in criminal record checks in Dalian.

This historical explanation should not be interpreted as confirmation that every provision of the 1990 regulation remains effective today.

Applying at the Correct District Office

Dalian has multiple district-level Exit and Entry Administration offices.

Applicants must submit their applications to the office responsible for the area where they lived in Dalian.

Applicants should confirm the appropriate service window before visiting.

Eligibility Based on Practical Experience

There are no publicly available written rules specifically setting out the eligibility requirements for foreign nationals applying for a Certificate of No Criminal Record in Dalian.

Based on practical application experience, applicants generally need to:

  • Have held residence permits issued in Dalian.
  • Have had Temporary Residence Registration Forms issued by local police stations in Dalian.
  • Have lived in Dalian for six months or longer.

Applicants should confirm their individual circumstances with the relevant public security authority.

Application Form

The relevant district Exit and Entry Administration office may require an application form when processing the application.

Applicants should confirm the current form and submission requirements with the relevant office.

The final notarial document includes an English translation or another language translation as requested by the applicant.

Applicants should understand this arrangement before submitting their original police-issued certificate.

7. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Where can foreigners apply for a Dalian Certificate of No Criminal Record?

Applications should be submitted to the district-level Exit and Entry Administration office of the Dalian Public Security Bureau that is responsible for the area where the applicant lived.

Do I need to obtain a Notary Introduction Letter before applying to the police?

The Introduction Letter may be required by the relevant district police office. However, according to the information provided for this guide, some police offices may no longer require it.

Applicants should contact the relevant district office to confirm the current requirement before visiting the notary office.

What are the eligibility requirements?

Based on practical application experience, foreign nationals generally need to have held residence permits issued in Dalian, have Temporary Residence Registration Forms issued by local police stations in Dalian, and have lived in Dalian for at least six months.

There are no publicly available written eligibility rules specifically addressing these requirements.

What documents are required for the application?

Applicants generally need their passports, a Power of Attorney if a third party is appointed, the Notary Introduction Letter if required, and the application form requested by the relevant district office.

How long does the entire procedure take?

According to the information provided for this guide:

  • Obtaining the Notary Introduction Letter: Usually on the spot.
  • Police application: Approximately 3–5 working days.
  • Final notarization: Approximately 3–5 working days.

The actual processing time may vary depending on the relevant authorities and notary office.

Where can I obtain the Notary Introduction Letter?

The Introduction Letter described in this guide is issued by Dalian Notary Public Office.

Applicants should first confirm whether the relevant district police office still requires this letter.

Will I get my original Certificate of No Criminal Record back after notarization?

Most notary offices will keep the original Certificate of No Criminal Record on file when processing the notarization application.

Applicants will generally receive a Notarial Certificate of No Criminal Record with the requested language translation instead of the original police-issued certificate.

Can I request an English translation?

Yes. The final Notarial Certificate of No Criminal Record can include an English translation or another language translation as requested by the applicant.

Does the notarized certificate need an apostille?

This depends on the destination country. Some countries require an apostille, while others may require consular legalization.

Applicants should confirm the requirements with the receiving authority before proceeding.
