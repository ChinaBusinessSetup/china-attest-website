About China Attest

China Attest is a service brand operated by Shenzhen Huanqiu Xuelv Technology Co., Ltd., a company established in Shenzhen, China. We help international clients navigate Chinese document procedures with clear guidance and dependable local support.

Our core services include assistance with Chinese police clearance certificates, notarisation, apostilles, and document authentication and legalisation. We assist with academic certificates and other personal and commercial documents, supporting clients both in China and overseas.

We understand that these documents often play an important role in employment, further study, immigration and other significant life decisions. Our role is to help you understand the requirements, prepare the necessary materials and coordinate the application process, keeping you informed along the way.

Every client’s circumstances are different. We take time to understand your needs and explain the available options, service scope, fees and expected timeframe before work begins. We value careful document handling, respect for your privacy and straightforward communication throughout the process.

Whether you are in China or managing your document requirements from overseas, China Attest provides a local point of contact to help you move forward.

Tell us what you need, and we will help you understand the next steps.
