Guide to Obtaining a Certificate of No Criminal Record in Xiamen, China

Learn how foreigners can obtain a Xiamen police clearance certificate, including eligibility, required documents, application procedures, notarization and apostille.

By: China Attest

Last Updated: 26 November 2026

1. Overview

Foreign nationals who have lived in Xiamen may need to obtain a Certificate of No Criminal Record for various purposes, including immigration, employment, and other overseas applications.

The certificate is also commonly referred to as a police certificate, police check, or police clearance certificate in other countries.

The procedures for obtaining a Certificate of No Criminal Record in Xiamen were updated following the implementation of the Provisions on the Handling of Criminal Record Checks by Public Security Organs, which came into effect on December 31, 2021.

The earlier Provisions for Fujian Public Security Police Stations on Issuance of Certificates of No Criminal Record (Trial Implementation) (2019) may no longer be effective.

For overseas use, the certificate generally needs to be notarized by a local notary office in China. Depending on the destination country, the notarized document may also require an apostille issued by China's Ministry of Foreign Affairs or consular legalization by the relevant embassy.

This guide explains the eligibility requirements, required documents, application procedures, and notarization process for foreign nationals seeking a Xiamen Certificate of No Criminal Record.

2. Eligibility

Foreign nationals applying for a Certificate of No Criminal Record in Xiamen must meet the following requirements:

  • Must have held residence permits issued in Xiamen.
  • Must have lived in Xiamen for 180 days or longer.

Applicants should prepare relevant documents to demonstrate their residence history and eligibility.

3. Required Documents

Step 1: Application for a Certificate of No Criminal Record

The following documents are required:

  • Passport(s): Relevant passport documents covering the applicant's residence history in Xiamen.
  • Power of Attorney: Required if a third party is appointed to handle the application on the applicant's behalf.

If the applicant is living outside China, the Power of Attorney must be either:

  • Apostilled by the competent foreign affairs authority in the applicant's country; or
  • Authenticated by a Chinese embassy or consulate in that country.

Step 2: Notarization of the Certificate of No Criminal Record

A notary office typically requires the following documents:

  • Passport(s).
  • Certificate of No Criminal Record: The certificate issued by the Xiamen Public Security Bureau.
  • Power of Attorney: Required if another person is appointed to handle the notarization application, where applicable.

Applicants should confirm the latest document requirements with their selected notary office before submission.

4. Application Process

Obtaining a Xiamen Certificate of No Criminal Record for overseas use generally involves two main steps.

Step 1: Apply for a Certificate of No Criminal Record

Foreign nationals can submit their applications to the Division of Exit and Entry Administration of the Xiamen Public Security Bureau.

Applicants can check the contact information of the relevant office through China's National Immigration Administration (NIA) website by selecting Xiamen.

Applicants should prepare their passports and, where applicable, a properly authenticated Power of Attorney if a third party is appointed.

Processing Time: Approximately 3 working days.

Step 2: Obtain Notarization of the Certificate

After receiving the Certificate of No Criminal Record from the Xiamen Public Security Bureau, applicants can submit it to a local notary office licensed to notarize Certificates of No Criminal Record for overseas use.

There are multiple notary offices in Xiamen providing foreign-related notarization services.

Xiamen Notary Public Office is one of the experienced options for this type of notarization.

Applicants can find contact information for notary offices throughout Xiamen through the 12348 Legal Services of Fujian website by selecting Xiamen.

Processing Time: Approximately 1 week.

Additional Authentication for Overseas Use

Depending on the destination country, the notarized document may also need:

  • Apostille: Issued by China's Ministry of Foreign Affairs or an authorized office.
  • Consular Legalization: Authentication by the relevant embassy when applicable.

The required authentication procedure depends on the destination country's document requirements.

5. Application Authority

Certificate of No Criminal Record

Applications can be submitted to:

English Name: Division of Exit and Entry Administration, Xiamen Public Security Bureau.

Chinese Name: 厦门市公安局出入境管理局.

Applicants can visit the National Immigration Administration (NIA) official website and select Xiamen to find the relevant office's contact information.

Applicants should confirm the current application arrangements and office information before visiting.

Notarization

There are multiple notary offices in Xiamen licensed to handle foreign-related notarization of Certificates of No Criminal Record for overseas use.

Recommended Option: Xiamen Notary Public Office.

Applicants can also consult the 12348 Legal Services of Fujian website and select Xiamen to find contact information for other notary offices.

Applicants may choose a suitable notary office according to their location and service requirements.

6. Special Requirements

Residence Permits Issued in Xiamen

Foreign nationals applying for a Xiamen Certificate of No Criminal Record must have held residence permits issued in Xiamen and lived there for at least 180 days.

Applicants should prepare relevant passport and residence documents to demonstrate their eligibility.

Power of Attorney for Applicants Outside China

If an applicant is living outside China and appoints a third party to handle the application, the Power of Attorney must be properly authenticated.

It must either be apostilled by the competent foreign affairs authority in the applicant's country or authenticated by a Chinese embassy or consulate in that country.

Notarization for Overseas Use

A Certificate of No Criminal Record is generally not sufficient for overseas immigration, employment, or educational applications without notarization.

Applicants should submit the certificate to a local notary office licensed to handle foreign-related notarization.

Sample Notarial Certificate of No Criminal Record

Notary offices across China issue Notarial Certificates of No Criminal Record in a consistent format, according to the information provided for this guide.

Depending on the destination country, the notarized document may also require an apostille or consular legalization.

Applicants should confirm the requirements with the receiving authority before proceeding.

7. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Where can foreigners apply for a Xiamen Certificate of No Criminal Record?

Applications can be submitted to the Division of Exit and Entry Administration of the Xiamen Public Security Bureau.

Applicants can find the office's contact information through the National Immigration Administration website by selecting Xiamen.

What are the eligibility requirements?

Foreign nationals must have held residence permits issued in Xiamen and must have lived in Xiamen for at least 180 days.

What documents are required for the application?

Applicants generally need their passports and a Power of Attorney if a third party is appointed to handle the application.

Does the Power of Attorney need to be authenticated?

If the applicant is living outside China, the Power of Attorney must be either apostilled by the competent foreign affairs authority in the applicant's country or authenticated by a Chinese embassy or consulate in that country.

How long does the application take?

The Certificate of No Criminal Record application generally takes approximately 3 working days.

Notarization generally takes an additional 1 week.

Where can I notarize my Xiamen Certificate of No Criminal Record?

Applicants can choose from multiple notary offices in Xiamen that provide foreign-related notarization services.

Xiamen Notary Public Office is one of the experienced options.

Does the notarized certificate need an apostille?

This depends on the destination country. Some countries require an apostille, while others may require consular legalization.

Applicants should confirm the requirements with the receiving authority before proceeding.
