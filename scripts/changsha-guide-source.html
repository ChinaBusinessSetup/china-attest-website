Guide to Obtaining a Certificate of No Criminal Record in Changsha, China

Learn how foreigners can obtain a Changsha police clearance certificate, including eligibility, required documents, application procedures and notarization.

By: China Attest

Last Updated: 26 November 2026

1. Overview

Foreign nationals who have lived in Changsha may need to obtain a Certificate of No Criminal Record for various purposes, including immigration, employment, and other overseas applications.

The certificate is also commonly referred to as a police certificate, police check, or police clearance certificate in other countries.

For overseas use, a Certificate of No Criminal Record issued by Chinese public security authorities generally needs to be notarized by a local notary office in China. Depending on the destination country, the notarized document may also require an apostille issued by China's Ministry of Foreign Affairs or consular legalization by the relevant embassy.

This guide explains the eligibility requirements, required documents, application procedures, and notarization process for foreign nationals seeking a Changsha Certificate of No Criminal Record.

2. Eligibility

Foreign nationals applying for a Certificate of No Criminal Record in Changsha must meet the following requirements:

  • Must have held residence permits issued in Changsha, as evidenced by their Temporary Residence Registration Form(s).
  • Must have lived in Changsha for 180 days or longer.

Applicants should prepare their Temporary Residence Registration Forms and other relevant supporting documents to demonstrate their residence history in Changsha.

3. Required Documents

Step 1: Application for a Certificate of No Criminal Record

The following documents are required:

  • Passport(s): Relevant passport documents covering the applicant's residence history in Changsha.
  • Temporary Residence Registration Form(s): Documents evidencing the applicant's residence registration in Changsha.
  • Power of Attorney: Required if a third party is appointed to handle the application on the applicant's behalf.

Step 2: Notarization of the Certificate of No Criminal Record

The following documents are required:

  • Certificate of No Criminal Record: The certificate issued by the Changsha Public Security Bureau.
  • Passport(s).
  • Power of Attorney: Required if another person is appointed to submit the notarization application.
  • Photograph of the Applicant Holding the Signed Power of Attorney: Required when another person is appointed to handle the notarization application, according to the requirements provided for this guide.

Applicants should confirm the latest document requirements with their selected notary office before submission.

4. Application Process

Obtaining a Changsha Certificate of No Criminal Record for overseas use generally involves two main steps.

Step 1: Apply for a Certificate of No Criminal Record

Foreign nationals may submit their applications to the Division of Exit and Entry Administration of the Changsha Public Security Bureau.

Applicants should prepare their passports, Temporary Residence Registration Forms, and a Power of Attorney if a third party is appointed.

Processing Time: Approximately 3–5 working days.

Step 2: Obtain Notarization of the Certificate

After receiving the Certificate of No Criminal Record from the Changsha Public Security Bureau, applicants can submit it to a local notary office for the issuance of a Notarial Certificate of No Criminal Record.

There are multiple notary offices in Changsha that provide notarization services for documents intended for overseas use.

Recommended Option: Changsha Notary Public Office, described in the original information for this guide as one of the most experienced options.

Applicants should confirm the current foreign-related notarization services and document requirements with their selected notary office before submission.

Processing Time: Approximately 1–2 weeks.

Additional Authentication for Overseas Use

Depending on the destination country, the notarized document may also need:

  • Apostille: Issued by China's Ministry of Foreign Affairs or an authorized office.
  • Consular Legalization: Authentication by the relevant embassy when applicable.

The required authentication procedure depends on the destination country's document requirements.

5. Application Authority

Certificate of No Criminal Record

Applications can be submitted to:

English Name: Division of Exit and Entry Administration, Changsha Public Security Bureau.

Applicants should confirm the current application arrangements and contact information with the relevant public security authority before visiting.

Notarization

There are multiple notary offices in Changsha that can notarize Certificates of No Criminal Record for overseas use.

Recommended Option: Changsha Notary Public Office.

According to the original information provided for this guide, it is one of the most experienced notary offices in Changsha for this type of service.

Applicants may contact a local notary office to confirm its foreign-related notarization services, required documents, and processing arrangements.

6. Special Requirements

Temporary Residence Registration Forms

Foreign nationals applying for a Changsha Certificate of No Criminal Record must provide Temporary Residence Registration Forms as evidence of their residence registration in Changsha.

These forms also support the applicant's eligibility to apply for the certificate.

Applicants should prepare the relevant forms covering their residence history.

Residence Permits Issued in Changsha

Applicants must have held residence permits issued in Changsha and lived in the city for at least 180 days.

Applicants should ensure that their residence registration documents support their application.

Power of Attorney for Third-Party Applications

If another person is appointed to submit the application for a Certificate of No Criminal Record, a Power of Attorney is required.

Applicants should prepare the authorization document before submitting the application.

Photograph of the Applicant Holding the Signed Power of Attorney

For the notarization application, if another person is appointed to handle the process, the applicant must also provide a photograph of themselves holding the signed Power of Attorney, according to the requirements stated in this guide.

This is an additional document requirement for third-party notarization applications and should not be confused with the Power of Attorney itself.

Applicants should confirm the latest requirements with their selected notary office before submission.

Sample Notarial Certificate of No Criminal Record

According to the original information provided for this guide, notary offices across China issue Notarial Certificates of No Criminal Record in a consistent format.

Notarization for Overseas Use

A Certificate of No Criminal Record is generally not sufficient for overseas immigration, employment, or educational applications without notarization.

Applicants should submit the certificate to a local notary office licensed to handle foreign-related notarization.

Additional Authentication for Overseas Use

Depending on the destination country, the notarized document may also require an apostille or consular legalization.

Applicants should confirm the requirements with the receiving authority before proceeding.

7. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Where can foreigners apply for a Changsha Certificate of No Criminal Record?

Applications can be submitted to the Division of Exit and Entry Administration of the Changsha Public Security Bureau.

Applicants should confirm the current application arrangements and contact information with the relevant public security authority.

What are the eligibility requirements?

Foreign nationals must have held residence permits issued in Changsha, as evidenced by their Temporary Residence Registration Forms, and must have lived in Changsha for at least 180 days.

Are Temporary Residence Registration Forms required?

Yes. According to the information provided for this guide, applicants need to provide Temporary Residence Registration Forms as evidence of their residence registration in Changsha.

What documents are required for the application?

Applicants generally need their passports, Temporary Residence Registration Forms, and a Power of Attorney if a third party is appointed to handle the application.

How long does the application take?

The Certificate of No Criminal Record application generally takes approximately 3–5 working days.

Notarization generally takes an additional 1–2 weeks.

Where can I notarize my Changsha Certificate of No Criminal Record?

There are multiple notary offices in Changsha that provide notarization services for overseas use.

Changsha Notary Public Office is one of the experienced options mentioned in this guide.

Can someone else submit the notarization application on my behalf?

Yes. A third party may be appointed to submit the notarization application using a Power of Attorney.

According to the requirements provided for this guide, the applicant must also provide a photograph of themselves holding the signed Power of Attorney.

Does the notarized certificate need an apostille?

This depends on the destination country. Some countries require an apostille, while others may require consular legalization.

Applicants should confirm the requirements with the receiving authority before proceeding.
