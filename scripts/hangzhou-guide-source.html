Guide to Obtaining a Certificate of No Criminal Record in Hangzhou, China

Learn how foreigners can obtain a Hangzhou police clearance certificate, including eligibility, required documents, power of attorney requirements and notarization.

By: China Attest

Last Updated: 26 November 2026

1. Overview

Foreign nationals who have lived in Hangzhou may need to obtain a Certificate of No Criminal Record for various purposes, including immigration, employment, and other overseas applications.

The certificate is also commonly referred to as a police certificate, police check, or police clearance certificate in other countries.

The procedures for obtaining a Certificate of No Criminal Record in Hangzhou are governed by two important regulations:

  • The Provisions on the Handling of Criminal Record Checks by Public Security Organs (2022), issued by China's Ministry of Public Security.
  • The Provisions on the Handling of Criminal Record Checks by the Public Security Organs of Zhejiang Province (2022), issued by the Zhejiang Provincial Public Security Department.

These two regulations are similar in most respects. However, the Zhejiang provincial regulation provides additional details, including specific requirements for a Power of Attorney.

For overseas use, the certificate generally needs to be notarized by a local notary office in China. Depending on the destination country, the notarized document may also require an apostille issued by China's Ministry of Foreign Affairs or consular legalization by the relevant embassy.

This guide explains the eligibility requirements, required documents, application procedures, and notarization process for foreign nationals seeking a Hangzhou Certificate of No Criminal Record.

2. Eligibility

Foreign nationals applying for a Certificate of No Criminal Record in Hangzhou must meet the following requirement:

  • Must have lived in Hangzhou for 180 days or longer.

Applicants should prepare relevant documents to demonstrate their residence history and registration in Hangzhou.

3. Required Documents

Step 1: Application for a Certificate of No Criminal Record

The following documents may be required:

  • Passport(s): Relevant passport documents covering the applicant's residence history in Hangzhou.
  • Power of Attorney: Required if a third party is appointed to handle the application on the applicant's behalf.
  • Notary Office's Criminal Record Investigation Request (Optional): This document can be obtained from a local notary office in Hangzhou. If the applicant provides an authenticated Power of Attorney, this additional document may not be necessary.

If the applicant is living outside China and appoints a third party to handle the application, the Power of Attorney generally needs to be authenticated by a Chinese embassy or consulate.

However, according to the practical requirements described in this guide, some police officers may waive this authentication requirement if the applicant provides a notary office's Criminal Record Investigation Request.

When this request is submitted, the police may send the criminal record check result directly to the relevant notary office.

Step 2: Notarization of the Certificate of No Criminal Record

The following documents are required:

  • Passport(s).
  • Certificate of No Criminal Record: The certificate issued by the relevant Hangzhou public security authority.
  • Power of Attorney: Required if a third party is appointed to handle the notarization application, where applicable.

Applicants should confirm the latest document requirements with their selected notary office before submission.

4. Application Process

Obtaining a Hangzhou Certificate of No Criminal Record for overseas use generally involves two main steps.

Step 1: Apply for a Certificate of No Criminal Record

Foreign nationals must submit their applications to the district branch office of the Division of Exit and Entry Administration of the Hangzhou Public Security Bureau that has jurisdiction over the district where they have lived and registered their residence.

The relevant district branch office is determined by the applicant's residence registration details, which can be found in their Temporary Residence Registration Form.

Applicants can check the contact information of the Exit and Entry Administration branch offices in Hangzhou through China's National Immigration Administration (NIA) website by selecting Hangzhou.

Applicants should prepare their passports and, where applicable, an authenticated Power of Attorney or a Criminal Record Investigation Request issued by a local notary office.

Processing Time: Approximately 3 working days.

Step 2: Obtain Notarization of the Certificate

After receiving the Certificate of No Criminal Record from the Hangzhou public security authority, applicants can submit it to a local notary office licensed to handle foreign-related notarization.

There are multiple notary offices in Hangzhou that are authorized to notarize Certificates of No Criminal Record for overseas use.

Applicants can find contact information for notary offices through:

  • Zhejiang Notary Association.
  • Hangzhou Justice Bureau.
  • Official websites of individual notary offices, including Hangzhou Guoli Notary Public Office.

Processing Time: Approximately 1–2 weeks.

Additional Authentication for Overseas Use

Depending on the destination country, the notarized document may also need:

  • Apostille: Issued by China's Ministry of Foreign Affairs or an authorized office.
  • Consular Legalization: Authentication by the relevant embassy when applicable.

The required authentication procedure depends on the destination country's document requirements.

5. Application Authority

Certificate of No Criminal Record

Applications must be submitted to the district branch office of the Division of Exit and Entry Administration of the Hangzhou Public Security Bureau that has jurisdiction over the district where the applicant has lived and registered their residence.

Application Authority: District branch offices of the Division of Exit and Entry Administration, Hangzhou Public Security Bureau.

The appropriate district office is determined by the applicant's residence registration details, which can be found in their Temporary Residence Registration Form.

Applicants can visit the National Immigration Administration (NIA) official website and select Hangzhou to find contact information for the relevant district offices.

Notarization

There are multiple notary offices in Hangzhou licensed to handle foreign-related notarization of Certificates of No Criminal Record for overseas use.

Applicants can obtain contact information through:

  1. Zhejiang Notary Association.
  2. Hangzhou Justice Bureau.
  3. Official websites of individual notary offices.

Example: Hangzhou Guoli Notary Public Office.

Applicants may choose a suitable notary office according to their location and service requirements.

6. Special Requirements

Applying at the Correct District Office

An important requirement in Hangzhou is that the application must be submitted to the district branch office that has jurisdiction over the area where the applicant has lived and registered their residence.

Applicants should check their Temporary Residence Registration Forms to identify the relevant district before submitting their applications.

Power of Attorney for Applicants Outside China

If an applicant is living outside China and appoints a third party to handle the application, the Power of Attorney generally needs to be authenticated by a Chinese embassy or consulate.

However, some police officers may waive the authentication requirement if the applicant provides a Criminal Record Investigation Request issued by a local notary office in Hangzhou.

Applicants should confirm which option is accepted by the relevant district office before arranging the authorization documents.

Notary Office's Criminal Record Investigation Request

A Criminal Record Investigation Request can be obtained from a local notary office in Hangzhou.

This document is optional if the applicant already has an authenticated Power of Attorney.

If the applicant submits the notary office's request, the police may send the criminal record check result directly to the relevant notary office.

This can help coordinate the application between the public security authority and the notary office.

Sample Notarial Certificate of No Criminal Record

According to the original information provided for this guide, notary offices across China issue Notarial Certificates of No Criminal Record in a consistent format.

For more information, please refer to the related article:

Notarization for Overseas Use

A Certificate of No Criminal Record is generally not sufficient for overseas immigration, employment, or educational applications without notarization.

Applicants should submit the certificate to a local notary office licensed to handle foreign-related notarization.

Additional Authentication for Overseas Use

Depending on the destination country, the notarized document may also require an apostille or consular legalization.

Applicants should confirm the requirements with the receiving authority before proceeding.

7. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Where can foreigners apply for a Hangzhou Certificate of No Criminal Record?

Applications must be submitted to the district branch office of the Division of Exit and Entry Administration of the Hangzhou Public Security Bureau that has jurisdiction over the district where the applicant has lived and registered their residence.

What are the eligibility requirements?

Foreign nationals must have lived in Hangzhou for at least 180 days.

Which district office should I visit?

You should apply at the district branch office that has jurisdiction over the area where your residence is registered.

Your Temporary Residence Registration Form can help identify the relevant district.

What documents are required for the application?

Applicants generally need their passports and a Power of Attorney if a third party is appointed.

A notary office's Criminal Record Investigation Request may also be provided as an optional alternative document.

Does my Power of Attorney need to be authenticated if I am outside China?

Generally, yes. The Power of Attorney must be authenticated by a Chinese embassy or consulate.

However, some police officers may waive this requirement if you provide a Criminal Record Investigation Request issued by a local notary office.

Applicants should confirm the accepted documentation with the relevant district office.

Is the Criminal Record Investigation Request mandatory?

No. According to the information in this guide, this document is optional if the applicant already has an authenticated Power of Attorney.

How long does the application take?

The Certificate of No Criminal Record application generally takes approximately 3 working days.

Notarization generally takes an additional 1–2 weeks.

Where can I notarize my Hangzhou Certificate of No Criminal Record?

Applicants can choose from multiple notary offices in Hangzhou licensed to handle foreign-related notarization.

Contact information is available through the Zhejiang Notary Association, Hangzhou Justice Bureau, and individual notary office websites.

Hangzhou Guoli Notary Public Office is one example.

Does the notarized certificate need an apostille?

This depends on the destination country. Some countries require an apostille, while others may require consular legalization.

Applicants should confirm the requirements with the receiving authority before proceeding.

  6. Hangzhou Guoli Notary Public Office – Official information about foreign-related notarization services.

Applicants should consult the relevant Hangzhou public security authorities and notary offices to confirm the latest application requirements, Power of Attorney authentication requirements, and document requirements before submitting their applications.
