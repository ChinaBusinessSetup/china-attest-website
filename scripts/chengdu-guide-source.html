Guide to Obtaining a Certificate of No Criminal Record in Chengdu, China

Learn how foreigners can obtain a Chengdu police clearance certificate, including eligibility, required documents, district application offices, notarization and apostille.

By: China Attest

Last Updated: 26 November 2026

1. Overview

Foreign nationals who have lived in Chengdu may need to obtain a Certificate of No Criminal Record for various purposes, including immigration, employment, and other overseas applications.

The certificate is also commonly referred to as a police certificate, police check, or police clearance certificate in other countries.

The procedures and requirements for obtaining a Certificate of No Criminal Record in Chengdu were updated in March 2022 following the implementation of the Provisions on the Handling of Criminal Record Checks by Public Security Organs, which came into effect on December 31, 2021.

The earlier Announcement Regarding the Issuance of Certificates of No Criminal Record to Foreigners in Chengdu (2016) is no longer effective.

For overseas use, the certificate generally needs to be notarized by a local notary office in China. Depending on the destination country, the notarized document may also require an apostille issued by China's Ministry of Foreign Affairs or consular legalization by the relevant embassy.

This guide explains the eligibility requirements, required documents, application procedures, and notarization process for foreign nationals seeking a Chengdu Certificate of No Criminal Record.

2. Eligibility

Foreign nationals applying for a Certificate of No Criminal Record in Chengdu must meet the following requirements:

  • Must have held residence permits issued in Chengdu.
  • Must have lived in Chengdu for 180 days or longer.

Applicants should prepare relevant documents to demonstrate their residence history and registration in Chengdu.

3. Required Documents

Step 1: Application for a Certificate of No Criminal Record

The following documents may be required:

  • Passport(s): Relevant passport documents covering the applicant's residence history in Chengdu.
  • Power of Attorney: Required if a third party is appointed to handle the application on the applicant's behalf.
  • Temporary Residence Registration Form(s): These may be requested by the handling officer. Some officers may not require this document.

If the applicant is living outside China and appoints a third party to handle the application, the Power of Attorney must be either:

  • Apostilled by the competent foreign affairs authority in the applicant's country; or
  • Authenticated by a Chinese embassy or consulate in that country.

Step 2: Notarization of the Certificate of No Criminal Record

The following documents are required:

  • Passport(s).
  • Certificate of No Criminal Record: The certificate issued by the relevant Chengdu public security authority.
  • Power of Attorney: Required if a third party is appointed to handle the notarization application.

Applicants should confirm the latest document requirements with their selected notary office before submission.

4. Application Process

Obtaining a Chengdu Certificate of No Criminal Record for overseas use generally involves two main steps.

Step 1: Apply for a Certificate of No Criminal Record

Foreign nationals must submit their applications to the district branch office of the Division of Exit and Entry Administration of the Chengdu Public Security Bureau that has jurisdiction over the district where they have lived and registered their residence.

The relevant district branch office is determined by the applicant's residence registration details, which can be found in their Temporary Residence Registration Form.

Applicants can check the contact information of the Exit and Entry Administration branch offices in Chengdu through China's National Immigration Administration (NIA) website by selecting Chengdu.

Applicants should prepare their passports, a Power of Attorney if a third party is appointed, and their Temporary Residence Registration Forms if requested.

Processing Time: Approximately 1–3 working days.

Step 2: Obtain Notarization of the Certificate

After receiving the Certificate of No Criminal Record from the public security authority, applicants can submit it to a local notary office for the issuance of a Notarial Certificate of No Criminal Record.

There are multiple notary offices in Chengdu licensed to notarize Certificates of No Criminal Record for overseas use.

Chengdu Notary Public Office is one of the experienced options for foreign-related notarization.

Applicants can find contact information for notary offices throughout Chengdu through the 12348 Sichuan Public Legal Service Website by selecting Chengdu.

Processing Time: Approximately 1–2 weeks.

Additional Authentication for Overseas Use

Depending on the destination country, the notarized document may also need:

  • Apostille: Issued by China's Ministry of Foreign Affairs or an authorized office.
  • Consular Legalization: Authentication by the relevant embassy when applicable.

The required authentication procedure depends on the destination country's document requirements.

5. Application Authority

Certificate of No Criminal Record

Applications must be submitted to the district branch office of the Division of Exit and Entry Administration of the Chengdu Public Security Bureau that has jurisdiction over the area where the applicant has lived and registered their residence.

The appropriate district branch office is determined by the applicant's residence registration details.

Application Authority: District branch offices of the Division of Exit and Entry Administration, Chengdu Public Security Bureau.

Official Information: National Immigration Administration of China (NIA).

Applicants can select Chengdu on the NIA website to find contact information for the relevant district offices.

Notarization

There are multiple notary offices in Chengdu licensed to handle foreign-related notarization of Certificates of No Criminal Record for overseas use.

Recommended Option: Chengdu Notary Public Office.

Applicants can also consult the 12348 Sichuan Public Legal Service Website for contact information and details of other notary offices in Chengdu.

Applicants may choose a suitable notary office according to their location and service requirements.

6. Special Requirements

Applying at the Correct District Office

An important requirement in Chengdu is that the application must be submitted to the district branch office that has jurisdiction over the area where the applicant has lived and registered their residence.

Applicants should check their Temporary Residence Registration Forms to identify the relevant district before submitting an application.

Temporary Residence Registration Forms

Temporary Residence Registration Forms provide details of an applicant's residence registration in Chengdu.

According to the information provided for this guide, some handling officers may require these forms, while others may not.

Applicants are advised to prepare the forms where available to help demonstrate their residence history.

Power of Attorney for Applicants Outside China

If an applicant is living outside China and appoints a third party to handle the application, the Power of Attorney must be properly authenticated.

It must either be apostilled by the competent foreign affairs authority in the applicant's country or authenticated by a Chinese embassy or consulate in that country, as applicable.

Notarization for Overseas Use

A Certificate of No Criminal Record is generally not sufficient for overseas immigration, employment, or educational applications without notarization.

Applicants should submit the certificate to a local notary office licensed to handle foreign-related notarization.

Sample Notarial Certificate of No Criminal Record

Notary offices across China issue Notarial Certificates of No Criminal Record in a consistent format, according to the information provided for this guide.

7. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Where can foreigners apply for a Chengdu Certificate of No Criminal Record?

Applications must be submitted to the district branch office of the Division of Exit and Entry Administration of the Chengdu Public Security Bureau that has jurisdiction over the district where the applicant has lived and registered their residence.

What are the eligibility requirements?

Foreign nationals must have held residence permits issued in Chengdu and must have lived in Chengdu for at least 180 days.

Do I need a Temporary Residence Registration Form?

The Temporary Residence Registration Form may be requested by the handling officer. According to the information in this guide, some officers do not require it.

Applicants are advised to prepare the form if available.

Can I apply at any Exit and Entry Administration office in Chengdu?

No. The application should be submitted to the district branch office that has jurisdiction over the district where the applicant has lived and registered their residence.

How long does the application take?

The application generally takes approximately 1–3 working days.

Notarization generally takes an additional 1–2 weeks.

Where can I notarize my Chengdu Certificate of No Criminal Record?

Applicants can choose from multiple notary offices in Chengdu that are licensed to handle foreign-related notarization.

Chengdu Notary Public Office is one of the experienced options.

Can someone apply on my behalf?

A third party may be appointed to handle the application using a Power of Attorney.

If the applicant is living outside China, the Power of Attorney must be apostilled or authenticated by a Chinese embassy or consulate, as applicable.

Does the notarized certificate need an apostille?

This depends on the destination country. Some countries require an apostille, while others may require consular legalization.

Applicants should confirm the requirements with the receiving authority before proceeding.
