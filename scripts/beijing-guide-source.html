Guide to Obtaining a Certificate of No Criminal Record in Beijing, China

Learn how foreigners can obtain a Beijing police clearance certificate, including eligibility, required documents, application procedures, notarization and translation.

By: China Attest

Last Updated: 26 November 2026

1. Overview

Foreign nationals who have lived in Beijing may need to obtain a Certificate of No Criminal Record for various purposes, including immigration, employment, and other overseas applications.

The certificate is also commonly referred to as a police certificate, police check, or police clearance certificate in other countries.

The procedures and requirements for obtaining a Certificate of No Criminal Record in Beijing are governed by the Implementation Rules for Handling Criminal Record Checks by Beijing Public Security Bureau, issued on March 25, 2022.

For overseas use, the certificate generally needs to be notarized and translated by a local notary office in China. Depending on the destination country, the notarized document may also require an apostille issued by China's Ministry of Foreign Affairs or consular legalization by the relevant embassy.

This guide explains the eligibility requirements, required documents, application procedures, and notarization process for foreign nationals seeking a Beijing Certificate of No Criminal Record.

2. Eligibility

Foreign nationals applying for a Certificate of No Criminal Record in Beijing must meet the following requirements:

  • Must have held residence permits issued in Beijing.
  • Must have lived in Beijing for 180 days or longer.

Applicants should prepare relevant documents to demonstrate their residence history in Beijing.

3. Required Documents

Step 1: Application for a Certificate of No Criminal Record

The following documents may be required:

  • Passport(s): Relevant passport documents covering the applicant's residence history in Beijing.
  • Temporary Residence Registration Forms (Optional): These may be provided as supporting documents.
  • Power of Attorney: Required if a third party is appointed to handle the application, where applicable.

Step 2: Notarization of the Certificate of No Criminal Record

The following documents are required:

  • Passport(s).
  • Certificate of No Criminal Record: The certificate issued by the relevant Beijing public security authority.
  • Power of Attorney: Required if another person is appointed to handle the notarization application, where applicable.

Applicants should confirm the latest document requirements with their selected notary office before submission.

4. Application Process

Obtaining a Beijing Certificate of No Criminal Record for overseas use generally involves two main steps.

Step 1: Apply for a Certificate of No Criminal Record

Foreign nationals can apply for a Certificate of No Criminal Record at one of the district branch offices of the Exit and Entry Administration of the Beijing Public Security Bureau (PSB).

Applicants can check the contact information of the relevant district branch offices through China's National Immigration Administration (NIA) website by selecting Beijing.

Applicants should prepare their passports and, where applicable, Temporary Residence Registration Forms and a Power of Attorney.

Processing Time: Approximately 3-5 working days.

Step 2: Obtain Notarization and Translation of the Certificate

After receiving the Certificate of No Criminal Record from the public security authority, applicants need to submit it to a local notary office for notarization and translation.

There are multiple notary offices in Beijing licensed to notarize Certificates of No Criminal Record for overseas use.

Beijing Fangyuan Notary Public Office is one of the experienced options for foreign-related notarization.

Applicants can find contact information for notary offices throughout Beijing through the 12348 Beijing Public Legal Service Website.

Processing Time: Approximately 1–2 weeks.

Additional Authentication for Overseas Use

Depending on the destination country, the notarized document may also need:

  • Apostille: Issued by China's Ministry of Foreign Affairs or an authorized office.
  • Consular Legalization: Authentication by the relevant embassy when applicable.

The required authentication procedure depends on the destination country's document requirements.

5. Application Authority

Certificate of No Criminal Record

Applications can be submitted to one of the district branch offices of the Exit and Entry Administration of the Beijing Public Security Bureau.

Applicants can obtain the contact information of the relevant district branch office through the National Immigration Administration (NIA) website by selecting Beijing.

Application Authority: District branch offices of the Exit and Entry Administration of Beijing Public Security Bureau.

Official Information: National Immigration Administration of China (NIA).

Applicants should confirm the appropriate district office and its current application arrangements before visiting.

Notarization

There are multiple notary offices in Beijing licensed to handle foreign-related notarization of Certificates of No Criminal Record for overseas use.

Recommended Option: Beijing Fangyuan Notary Public Office (北京方圆公证处).

Applicants can also consult the 12348 Beijing Public Legal Service Website for contact information and details of other notary offices in Beijing.

Applicants may choose a suitable notary office according to their location and service requirements.

6. Special Requirements

Temporary Residence Registration Forms

Temporary Residence Registration Forms are listed as optional supporting documents for the Beijing application.

Applicants may prepare relevant forms covering their residence history in Beijing where available.

Applying Through a Third Party

If an applicant appoints another person to handle the application or notarization, a Power of Attorney may be required.

Applicants should confirm the applicable authorization requirements with the relevant public security authority or notary office before submission.

Notarization and Translation for Overseas Use

A Certificate of No Criminal Record is generally not sufficient for overseas immigration, employment, or educational applications without notarization.

The certificate should be submitted to a qualified local notary office for notarization and translation.

Sample Notarial Certificate of No Criminal Record

Notary offices across China issue Notarial Certificates of No Criminal Record in a consistent format, according to the information provided for this guide.

For more information, please refer to the related article:

Additional Authentication

Applicants should check the requirements of their destination country to determine whether the notarized document also needs an apostille or consular legalization.

7. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Where can foreigners apply for a Beijing Certificate of No Criminal Record?

Foreign nationals can apply at one of the district branch offices of the Exit and Entry Administration of the Beijing Public Security Bureau.

The National Immigration Administration website provides contact information for the relevant offices. Select Beijing to find the appropriate information.

What are the eligibility requirements?

Foreign nationals must have held residence permits issued in Beijing and must have lived in Beijing for at least 180 days.

Are Temporary Residence Registration Forms mandatory?

According to the information in this guide, Temporary Residence Registration Forms are optional supporting documents for the application.

How long does the application take?

The application generally takes approximately 3-5 working days.

Notarization and translation generally take an additional 1–2 weeks.

Where can I notarize my Beijing Certificate of No Criminal Record?

Applicants can choose from multiple notary offices in Beijing that are licensed to handle foreign-related notarization.

Beijing Fangyuan Notary Public Office is one of the experienced options.

Can someone apply on my behalf?

A third party may be appointed to handle the application or notarization where permitted. A Power of Attorney may be required.

Applicants should confirm the specific authorization requirements with the relevant authority or notary office.

Does the notarized certificate need an apostille?

This depends on the destination country. Some countries require an apostille, while others may require consular legalization.

Applicants should confirm the requirements with the receiving authority before proceeding.

8. Official Sources

The following regulations and official resources are relevant to the Beijing application and notarization procedures:

  1. Implementation Rules for Handling Criminal Record Checks by Beijing Public Security Bureau, issued on March 25, 2022.
  2. National Immigration Administration of China (NIA) – Information about district Exit and Entry Administration offices in Beijing.
  3. 12348 Beijing Public Legal Service Website – Contact information for notary offices in Beijing.

Applicants should consult the relevant Beijing public security authorities and notary offices to confirm the latest application requirements, office information, and document requirements before submitting their applications.
