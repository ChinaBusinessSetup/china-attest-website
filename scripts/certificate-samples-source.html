1. Overview

A China Certificate of No Criminal Record is an official document issued by Chinese public security authorities confirming an individual's criminal record status in China for a specified period. It is also commonly known as a China police certificate, police check, or police clearance certificate.

Foreign nationals who have lived in China may need this document for immigration, employment, adoption, school admission, professional licensing, or other overseas applications. Popular destinations include the United States, Canada, Australia, the United Kingdom, and New Zealand.

However, there is an important distinction between a Certificate of No Criminal Record issued by the local Public Security Bureau (PSB) and a Notarial Certificate of No Criminal Record issued by a Chinese notary office.

For many overseas applications, the police-issued certificate needs to be submitted to a local Chinese notary office for notarization and foreign-language translation. Depending on the destination country and the receiving authority's requirements, the notarized document may also need an Apostille.

In this guide, we provide examples of Chinese police certificates, notarial certificates used for different immigration destinations, and apostilled documents. We also explain how to identify the appropriate document format for your application.

2. Police-issued Certificate vs. Notarial Certificate

One of the most important things to understand when applying for a China police clearance certificate is the difference between the original certificate issued by the Public Security Bureau and the Notarial Certificate of No Criminal Record issued by a Chinese notary office.

Certificate of No Criminal Record Issued by the Public Security Bureau

This is the original police certificate issued by the local Public Security Bureau or its Exit and Entry Administration Department. It confirms the applicant's criminal record status in China for the period stated in the document.

Notarial Certificate of No Criminal Record

This document is issued by a local Chinese notary office. It generally contains the police-issued certificate or relevant supporting documentation, together with the required notarization and foreign-language translation.

The two documents serve different purposes and should not be treated as interchangeable. Applicants should confirm which format is required by the receiving immigration authority or other overseas institution.

3. Sample China Certificate of No Criminal Record

Sample Certificate of No Criminal Record Issued by Chinese Police

The Chinese Ministry of Public Security provides a standard format for Certificates of No Criminal Record under the Provisions on the Handling of Criminal Record Checks by Public Security Organs.

However, local public security authorities may issue certificates with variations in layout and presentation. For example, although the standard format includes a QR code, some local police authorities may issue certificates without one.

What Information Does the Certificate Contain?

The certificate may contain:

  • Applicant's personal information.
  • Identification or passport details.
  • The period covered by the criminal record check.
  • A statement concerning the applicant's criminal record.
  • The issuing public security authority.
  • An official seal.
  • A QR code, where included.

Does a China Police Certificate Need a QR Code?

Not necessarily. Although the national standard format includes a QR code, local public security authorities may issue certificates without one. According to the current IRCC China police certificate instructions, certificates without a QR code are also acceptable. Applicants should follow the document requirements of their receiving authority.

5. Apostille and Authentication of a China Notarial Certificate

Depending on the destination country and the requirements of the receiving authority, a Notarial Certificate of No Criminal Record may need additional authentication before it can be used overseas.

What Is an Apostille?

An Apostille is a certificate issued by a designated competent authority under the Hague Apostille Convention. It authenticates the origin of a public document, including the signature, the capacity in which the person signing acted, and the seal or stamp on the document.

China's Apostille Convention entered into force on November 7, 2023. Since then, eligible public documents issued in mainland China can generally be authenticated through the Apostille procedure for use in other countries where the Convention applies.

Learn about China Apostille Services.

Does Every China Police Clearance Certificate Need an Apostille?

No. The requirement depends on the destination country, the receiving institution, and the type of document being submitted. Applicants should confirm whether an Apostille is required before arranging authentication.

6. Important Things to Know

Does the Translation Page of a Notarial Certificate Need a Red Seal?

Not necessarily. Some applicants may be concerned when the foreign-language translation pages of a Chinese Notarial Certificate of No Criminal Record do not have a separate red official seal.

In our document-handling experience, we have encountered notarial certificates whose translation pages did not carry separate red seals. The absence of an individual red seal on every translation page does not automatically mean that the document is invalid.

Chinese notarial certificates may contain a separate translation certification or other notarial authentication confirming that the translation corresponds to the original document.

Applicants should check the complete notarial document and confirm that its format meets the requirements of the receiving authority rather than judging its validity solely by whether every page carries a red seal.

How Long Is a China Certificate of No Criminal Record Valid?

There is no single validity period that applies to every overseas immigration application. The validity of a police-issued Certificate of No Criminal Record and the acceptance period of a Notarial Certificate of No Criminal Record should be considered separately.

Police-issued Certificate of No Criminal Record

The receiving authority may require the certificate to have been issued within a specific period before the application is submitted. The applicable time limit depends on the destination country and the immigration program.

The police sample in this guide contains its own three-month validity wording. That wording relates to that particular certificate and should not be treated as a universal overseas acceptance period.

Notarial Certificate of No Criminal Record

A notarized document does not necessarily have a universal expiration date. However, this does not mean it will be accepted indefinitely for every immigration application. Receiving authorities may impose their own document freshness requirements or request updated evidence during application processing.

Our Recommendation

Before applying for a China police certificate, check the latest document validity requirements of the immigration authority receiving your application.

Why Do Notarial Certificates Have Different Formats?

Chinese notary offices may issue Notarial Certificates of No Criminal Record in different formats. The format may vary depending on:

  • The destination country.
  • The requirements of the receiving immigration authority.
  • Whether a copy of the original police certificate needs to be included.
  • The required foreign-language translation.
  • Whether additional translation certification or authentication is necessary.

For this reason, applicants should determine the intended use of the document before submitting their notarization application. Obtaining the correct format at the beginning can help reduce unnecessary revisions and additional processing time.

Should I Obtain the Correct Notarial Format Before Applying?

Yes. Applicants should identify their intended destination country and check the receiving authority's document requirements before arranging notarization. This helps ensure that the document format, translation, and any required supporting pages are prepared appropriately.

7. Frequently Asked Questions

What does a China Police Certificate look like?

A China Certificate of No Criminal Record is issued by a local Chinese public security authority. Its format may vary between cities, but it generally identifies the applicant and states the criminal record check result for a specified period.

What is the difference between a police certificate and a notarial certificate?

A police certificate is issued by the Public Security Bureau, while a Notarial Certificate of No Criminal Record is issued by a Chinese notary office and generally includes the relevant notarization and foreign-language translation.

Does a China Police Certificate need a QR code?

Not necessarily. Some local public security authorities issue certificates without a QR code. Applicants should follow the current requirements of their receiving authority.

Does a China Police Certificate need notarization?

For many overseas applications, the police-issued certificate needs to be notarized and translated by a Chinese notary office. However, applicants should confirm the specific requirements of the receiving institution.

Which notarial format is used for Canadian immigration?

China Attest recommends considering a format that includes a copy of the original police-issued certificate as part of the notarial document. Applicants must also check their current IRCC instructions and individual document checklist.

Which format is used for U.S. immigration?

The sample in this guide shows a format that includes additional pages certifying the accuracy of the English translation. Applicants should check the U.S. Department of State's current China Reciprocity Schedule and the requirements for their visa category.

Does a China Notarial Certificate need an Apostille?

Not always. This depends on the destination country, receiving institution, and applicable international document authentication requirements.

Does the translation page need a red official seal?

Not necessarily. The absence of an individual red seal on every translation page does not automatically make the document invalid. The complete notarial certification should be reviewed.

How long is a China Police Certificate valid?

There is no single validity period for every overseas application. The receiving authority determines how recently the certificate must have been issued.

Can I obtain a China Police Certificate while living abroad?

In many circumstances, foreign nationals may arrange applications through an authorized representative, depending on the city, local public security requirements, and authorization documents. Applicants should check the relevant city's application procedures.
