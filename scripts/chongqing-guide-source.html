Guide to Obtaining a Certificate of No Criminal Record in Chongqing, China

Learn how foreigners can obtain a Chongqing police clearance certificate, including eligibility, residence requirements, required documents, application procedures and notarization.

By: China Attest

Last Updated: 26 November 2026

1. Overview

Foreign nationals who have lived in Chongqing may need to obtain a Certificate of No Criminal Record for various purposes, including immigration, employment, and other overseas applications.

The certificate is also commonly referred to as a police certificate, police check, or police clearance certificate in other countries.

For overseas use, a Certificate of No Criminal Record issued by local Chinese public security authorities generally needs to be notarized by a local Chinese notary office. Depending on the destination country, the notarized document may also require authentication or legalization by the relevant embassy or consulate.

This guide explains the eligibility requirements, required documents, application procedures, and notarization process for foreign nationals seeking a Chongqing Certificate of No Criminal Record.

2. Eligibility

There are no publicly available written rules specifying the eligibility requirements for obtaining a Certificate of No Criminal Record in Chongqing.

Based on practical experience handling applications in Chongqing, foreign nationals generally need to meet the following conditions:

  • Must have held residence permits issued in Chongqing.
  • Must have lived in Chongqing for six months or longer.

These requirements are based on practical application experience rather than publicly available written eligibility rules. Applicants should confirm their eligibility with the relevant district Exit and Entry Administration before submitting an application.

3. Required Documents

Step 1: Application for a Certificate of No Criminal Record

The following documents are generally required:

  • Passport(s): Relevant passport documents covering the applicant's residence history in Chongqing.
  • Power of Attorney: Required if a third party is appointed to handle the application on the applicant's behalf.

If the applicant is outside China, the Power of Attorney may need to be apostilled or authenticated, depending on the requirements of the relevant local Exit and Entry Administration office.

Step 2: Notarization of the Certificate of No Criminal Record

The following documents are generally required:

  • Passport(s).
  • Certificate of No Criminal Record: The certificate issued by the relevant Chongqing public security authority.
  • Power of Attorney: Required if a third party is appointed to handle the notarization application, where applicable.

Applicants should confirm the latest document requirements with their selected notary office before submission.

4. Application Process

Obtaining a Chongqing Certificate of No Criminal Record for overseas use generally involves two main steps.

Step 1: Apply for a Certificate of No Criminal Record

Foreign nationals who have lived in Chongqing may submit their applications to the local Exit and Entry Administration office of the Chongqing Public Security Bureau that has jurisdiction over their residence.

If an applicant has lived in multiple districts of Chongqing, the application should be submitted to the Exit and Entry Administration office in the district of their most recent residence.

The relevant district can be identified through the applicant's residence registration information.

The National Immigration Administration (NIA) provides contact information for Exit and Entry Administration offices throughout China. Applicants can visit its official website and select Chongqing to find the relevant district office.

Applicants should prepare their passports and a Power of Attorney if a third party is appointed.

If the applicant is outside China, the relevant district office should be consulted regarding whether the Power of Attorney needs to be apostilled or authenticated.

Processing Time: Approximately 1–3 working days.

Step 2: Obtain Notarization of the Certificate

After receiving the Certificate of No Criminal Record from the Chongqing public security authority, applicants can submit it to a local notary office for the issuance of a Notarial Certificate of No Criminal Record.

There are multiple notary offices in Chongqing licensed to issue Notarial Certificates of No Criminal Record for overseas use.

Recommended Option: Chongqing Notary Public Office.

Applicants should confirm the current service availability and document requirements with their selected notary office before submission.

Processing Time: Approximately 3–5 working days.

Additional Authentication or Legalization for Overseas Use

Depending on the destination country, the notarized document may also need authentication or legalization by the relevant embassy or consulate.

Applicants should check the receiving country's document requirements before proceeding.

For more information, please refer to China Attest's existing guide on document authentication and legalization.

5. Application Authority

Certificate of No Criminal Record

Applications should be submitted to the local Exit and Entry Administration office of the Chongqing Public Security Bureau that has jurisdiction over the applicant's residence.

If the applicant has lived in multiple districts, the relevant office is the one in the district of their most recent residence.

Application Authority: Local Exit and Entry Administration offices of Chongqing Public Security Bureau.

Official Information: National Immigration Administration of China (NIA).

Applicants can visit the NIA official website and select Chongqing to find contact information for the relevant district offices.

Notarization

There are multiple notary offices in Chongqing licensed to issue Notarial Certificates of No Criminal Record for overseas use.

Recommended Option: Chongqing Notary Public Office.

Applicants may contact a qualified local notary office to confirm its current foreign-related notarization services, required documents, and processing arrangements.

6. Special Requirements

Applying at the Correct District Office

An important consideration when applying in Chongqing is determining which district Exit and Entry Administration office has jurisdiction over the applicant's residence.

If an applicant has lived in multiple districts of Chongqing, the application should be submitted to the office in the district of their most recent residence.

Applicants should check their residence registration history before selecting the application office.

Eligibility Based on Practical Experience

Unlike some other cities, publicly available written eligibility rules specifically addressing foreign nationals' applications in Chongqing are not available according to the information provided for this guide.

Based on practical application experience, applicants generally need to have held residence permits issued in Chongqing and lived there for at least six months.

Applicants should confirm their individual eligibility with the relevant local authority.

Power of Attorney for Applicants Outside China

If an applicant is outside China and appoints a third party to handle the application, the Power of Attorney may need to be apostilled or authenticated.

The exact requirement depends on the relevant local Exit and Entry Administration office.

Applicants should confirm the applicable requirements with the district office before arranging authentication of their Power of Attorney.

Notarization and Original Certificate Retention

One important point to understand before applying for notarization is that most notary offices will keep the original Certificate of No Criminal Record on file.

This means applicants generally will no longer possess the original police-issued certificate after the notarization application has been submitted.

After notarization is completed, applicants receive a new document called a Notarial Certificate of No Criminal Record, which includes an English translation or another language translation as requested.

Applicants should confirm the specific document format and translation language with the notary office before submission.

Additional Authentication for Overseas Use

Depending on the destination country, the notarized document may also require authentication or legalization by the relevant embassy or consulate.

Applicants should confirm the requirements with the receiving authority before proceeding.

Sample Notarial Certificate of No Criminal Record

Notary offices across China issue Notarial Certificates of No Criminal Record in a consistent format, according to the information provided for this guide.

7. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Where can foreigners apply for a Chongqing Certificate of No Criminal Record?

Applications should be submitted to the local Exit and Entry Administration office of the Chongqing Public Security Bureau that has jurisdiction over the applicant's residence.

Which district office should I visit if I have lived in multiple districts of Chongqing?

If you have lived in multiple districts, you should generally apply at the Exit and Entry Administration office in the district of your most recent residence.

What are the eligibility requirements?

Based on practical application experience, foreign nationals generally need to have held residence permits issued in Chongqing and lived in Chongqing for at least six months.

There are no publicly available written eligibility rules specifically addressing these requirements, so applicants should confirm their eligibility with the relevant district office.

What documents are required for the application?

Applicants generally need their passports and a Power of Attorney if a third party is appointed to handle the application.

Additional authentication of the Power of Attorney may be required if the applicant is outside China. The specific requirements depend on the relevant district office.

How long does the application take?

The Certificate of No Criminal Record application generally takes approximately 1–3 working days.

Notarization generally takes an additional 3–5 working days.

Where can I notarize my Chongqing Certificate of No Criminal Record?

Applicants can choose from multiple notary offices in Chongqing licensed to issue Notarial Certificates of No Criminal Record for overseas use.

Chongqing Notary Public Office is one of the available options mentioned in this guide.

Will I get my original Certificate of No Criminal Record back after notarization?

Most notary offices will keep the original Certificate of No Criminal Record on file when processing the notarization application.

After notarization is completed, applicants receive a Notarial Certificate of No Criminal Record with an English translation or another requested language translation.

Does the notarized certificate need additional authentication?

This depends on the destination country. Some countries may require further authentication or legalization by the relevant embassy or consulate.

Applicants should confirm the requirements with the receiving authority before proceeding.
