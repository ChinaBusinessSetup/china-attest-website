Guide to Obtaining a Certificate of No Criminal Record in Shenyang, China

Learn how foreigners can obtain a Shenyang police clearance certificate, including eligibility, required documents, application procedures and notarization.

By: China Attest

Last Updated: 26 November 2026

1. Overview

Foreign nationals who have lived in Shenyang may need to obtain a Certificate of No Criminal Record for various purposes, including immigration, employment, and other overseas applications.

The certificate is also commonly referred to as a police certificate, police check, or police clearance certificate in other countries.

Foreign nationals may need to obtain a Certificate of No Criminal Record in China for different overseas purposes. For use abroad, the certificate generally needs to be notarized by a local notary office in China. Depending on the destination country, the notarized document may also require an apostille issued by China's Ministry of Foreign Affairs or consular legalization by the relevant embassy.

This guide explains the eligibility requirements, required documents, application procedures, and notarization process for foreign nationals seeking a Shenyang Certificate of No Criminal Record.

2. Eligibility

Foreign nationals may apply for a Certificate of No Criminal Record in Shenyang if they meet the following requirements:

  • Must have resided in Shenyang for more than six months.
  • Must have held residence permits issued in Shenyang.

Applicants should prepare supporting documents demonstrating their residence history and residence permit information in Shenyang.

3. Required Documents

Step 1: Application for a Certificate of No Criminal Record

According to the document requirements provided for this guide, applicants should prepare:

  • Current Passport: The applicant's current passport.
  • Previous Passport: Required if applicable.
  • Temporary Residence Registration Forms: Relevant residence registration documents covering the applicant's stay in Shenyang.
  • Written Application Statement: A written statement submitted as part of the application.
  • Power of Attorney: Required if a third party is appointed to handle the application, where applicable.

Step 2: Notarization of the Certificate of No Criminal Record

The following documents are required:

  • Passport(s).
  • Certificate of No Criminal Record: The certificate issued by the Shenyang Public Security Bureau.
  • Power of Attorney: Required if a third party is appointed to handle the notarization application.

Applicants should confirm the latest document requirements with their selected notary office before submission.

4. Application Process

Obtaining a Shenyang Certificate of No Criminal Record for overseas use generally involves two main steps.

Step 1: Apply for a Certificate of No Criminal Record

Foreign nationals who have lived in Shenyang may submit their applications to the Division of Exit and Entry Administration of the Shenyang Public Security Bureau.

Application Authority: Division of Exit and Entry Administration, Shenyang Public Security Bureau.

Address: No. 47 Beiling Street, Huanggu District, Shenyang, China.

Applicants should prepare their current passports, previous passports if applicable, Temporary Residence Registration Forms, a written application statement, and a Power of Attorney if a third party is appointed.

Processing Time: Approximately 1 week.

Step 2: Obtain Notarization of the Certificate

After receiving the Certificate of No Criminal Record from the Shenyang Public Security Bureau, applicants can submit it to a local notary office licensed to issue Notarial Certificates of No Criminal Record for overseas use.

One option is the Liaoning Notary Public Office, which is frequently used for this type of notarization according to the information provided for this guide.

Recommended Notary Office: Liaoning Notary Public Office.

Address: 8th Floor, Guangda Building, No. 77-2 Beizhan Road, Shenhe District, Shenyang, China.

Applicants may also choose another qualified notary office in Shenyang.

Processing Time: Approximately 3–5 working days.

Additional Authentication for Overseas Use

Depending on the destination country, the notarized document may also need:

  • Apostille: Issued by China's Ministry of Foreign Affairs or an authorized office.
  • Consular Legalization: Authentication by the relevant embassy when applicable.

The required authentication procedure depends on the destination country's document requirements.

5. Application Authority

Certificate of No Criminal Record

Applications can be submitted to:

Notarization

There are multiple notary offices in Shenyang licensed to issue Notarial Certificates of No Criminal Record for overseas use.

One option is:

English Name: Liaoning Notary Public Office.

Chinese Name: 辽宁省公证处.

Address (English): 8th Floor, Guangda Building, No. 77-2 Beizhan Road, Shenhe District, Shenyang, China.

Applicants may choose a suitable notary office according to their location and service requirements.

6. Special Requirements

Residence History in Shenyang

Foreign nationals applying for a Shenyang Certificate of No Criminal Record must have resided in Shenyang for more than six months and held residence permits issued in Shenyang.

Applicants should prepare their passport records and Temporary Residence Registration Forms to support their residence history.

Previous Passports

Applicants should provide copies or relevant documents relating to previous passports, where applicable.

These documents may help establish the applicant's identity and residence history during their stay in Shenyang.

Written Application Statement

A written application statement is listed among the required documents for applying for a Shenyang Certificate of No Criminal Record.

Applicants should prepare this statement as part of their application materials.

Power of Attorney for Third-Party Applications

If another person is appointed to handle the application, a Power of Attorney may be required.

Applicants should confirm the applicable authorization requirements with the Shenyang Public Security Bureau or the selected notary office before submission.

Notarization for Overseas Use

A Certificate of No Criminal Record is generally not sufficient for overseas immigration, employment, or educational applications without notarization.

Applicants should submit the certificate to a local notary office licensed to handle foreign-related notarization.

Sample Notarial Certificate of No Criminal Record

Notary offices across China issue Notarial Certificates of No Criminal Record in a consistent format, according to the information provided for this guide.

7. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Where can foreigners apply for a Shenyang Certificate of No Criminal Record?

Applications can be submitted to the Division of Exit and Entry Administration of the Shenyang Public Security Bureau at No. 47 Beiling Street, Huanggu District, Shenyang.

What are the eligibility requirements?

Foreign nationals must have resided in Shenyang for more than six months and must have held residence permits issued in Shenyang.

What documents are required for the application?

Applicants should prepare their current passports, previous passports if applicable, Temporary Residence Registration Forms, a written application statement, and a Power of Attorney if a third party is appointed.

How long does the application take?

The application generally takes approximately one week.

Notarization generally takes an additional 3–5 working days.

Where can I notarize my Shenyang Certificate of No Criminal Record?

Applicants can choose from multiple notary offices in Shenyang licensed to issue Notarial Certificates of No Criminal Record for overseas use.

Liaoning Notary Public Office is one of the options mentioned in this guide.

Do I need to provide previous passports?

Previous passports are required where applicable, according to the document requirements provided for this guide.

Do I need a written application statement?

Yes. A written application statement is included in the required documents for the Shenyang application.

Does the notarized certificate need an apostille?

This depends on the destination country. Some countries require an apostille, while others may require consular legalization.

Applicants should confirm the requirements with the receiving authority before proceeding.
