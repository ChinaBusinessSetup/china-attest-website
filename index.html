Can you help after I have left China?

We can review your case from overseas. Whether remote processing is possible depends on your city, residence records and the document required. Contact us before making arrangements.

How much does the service cost?

The quotation depends on the documents, location and work involved. Share your requirements so we can discuss the scope and price before you proceed.

How long will it take?

We discuss an estimated timeline after reviewing your case. Official processing and requests for additional records may affect completion; no universal deadline is promised.

What should I send in my first message?

Your document type, China city, relevant dates and destination country are a useful starting point. Please do not send passport numbers or other sensitive records in an initial inquiry.
