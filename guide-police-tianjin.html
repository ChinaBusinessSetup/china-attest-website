Guide to Obtaining a Certificate of No Criminal Record in Tianjin, China

Learn how foreigners can obtain a Tianjin police clearance certificate, including the required notary letter, eligibility, application documents and three-step notarization process.

By: China Attest

Last Updated: 26 November 2026

1. Overview

Foreign nationals who have lived in Tianjin may need to obtain a Certificate of No Criminal Record for various purposes, including immigration, employment, and other overseas applications.

The certificate is also commonly referred to as a police certificate, police check, or police clearance certificate in other countries.

Tianjin has a distinctive application procedure for foreign nationals seeking a Certificate of No Criminal Record.

Around the end of 2020, the Exit and Entry Administration offices in individual districts of Tianjin became responsible for issuing Certificates of No Criminal Record to foreign residents within their respective districts.

Previously, applications were handled by the Division of Exit and Entry Administration of the Tianjin Public Security Bureau (PSB). In 2017, the responsible police agency was the Tianjin Criminal Science & Technology Research Institute.

These changes reflect the development of the local procedures for handling criminal record check applications in Tianjin.

For overseas use, a Certificate of No Criminal Record generally needs to be notarized by a local notary office in China. Depending on the destination country, the notarized document may also require an apostille issued by China's Ministry of Foreign Affairs or consular legalization by the relevant embassy.

Unlike many other cities, the Tianjin application procedure generally involves three steps:

  1. Obtain a Request for Foreigner Criminal Record Check letter from a local notary office.
  2. Submit the notary's letter to the relevant district Exit and Entry Administration office and obtain the Certificate of No Criminal Record.
  3. Return the sealed certificate to the notary office for final notarization and translation.

This guide explains the eligibility requirements, required documents, application procedures, and notarization process for foreign nationals seeking a Tianjin Certificate of No Criminal Record.

2. Eligibility

The Tianjin procedure involves different eligibility requirements at different stages.

Step 1: Obtaining the Notary Letter

According to the information provided for this guide, any foreign national holding a valid passport may request a Request for Foreigner Criminal Record Check letter from a local notary office in Tianjin.

Step 2: Applying for a Certificate of No Criminal Record

Foreign nationals applying for the certificate through the local public security authority must generally meet the following requirements:

  • Must have Temporary Residence Registration Forms issued by local police stations in Tianjin.
  • Must have lived in Tianjin for 180 days or longer.

Applicants should confirm their eligibility and residence registration records with the relevant district Exit and Entry Administration office before proceeding.

3. Required Documents

The application involves three stages, each with its own document requirements.

Step 1: Obtaining the Notary Letter

The following documents are required:

  • Passport(s): The applicant's passport documents.
  • Power of Attorney: Required if a third party is appointed to handle the application, where applicable.

Step 2: Application for a Certificate of No Criminal Record

The following documents are required:

  • Passport(s).
  • Power of Attorney: Required if applicable.
  • Request for Foreigner Criminal Record Check Letter: The original letter issued by the local notary office and delivered in its sealed envelope.

Step 3: Final Notarization

The main document required is:

  • Sealed Certificate of No Criminal Record: The envelope containing the certificate issued by the relevant Tianjin public security authority.

The sealed envelope must be returned to the notary office for the final notarization procedure.

The notary office will then issue a Notarial Certificate of No Criminal Record with an English translation or another language translation as requested by the applicant.

Applicants should confirm any additional document requirements with their selected notary office.

4. Application Process

Obtaining a Tianjin Certificate of No Criminal Record for overseas use generally involves three main steps.

Step 1: Obtain a Request for Foreigner Criminal Record Check Letter

The first step is to visit a local notary office in Tianjin and request a letter titled:

Request for Foreigner Criminal Record Check

This letter is required when applying for a Certificate of No Criminal Record with the local public security authority.

The notary office will place the letter in a sealed envelope.

Applicants must deliver the sealed envelope to the district Exit and Entry Administration office responsible for their residence.

There are multiple notary offices in Tianjin.

One commonly used option is:

Tianjin Beifang Notary Public Office

According to the information provided for this guide, it is one of the largest notary offices in Tianjin.

Processing Time: Approximately 1–3 working days.

Step 2: Apply for a Certificate of No Criminal Record

After obtaining the notary's sealed letter, applicants can submit their applications to the district office of the Exit and Entry Administration of the Tianjin Public Security Bureau that has jurisdiction over their residence.

Applicants must apply through the district office responsible for their residence in Tianjin.

The Tianjin Public Security Bureau publishes contact information for the district offices.

Applicants should prepare their passports, Temporary Residence Registration Forms, and the sealed Request for Foreigner Criminal Record Check letter.

Processing Time: Approximately 3 business days.

Step 3: Return to the Notary Office for Final Notarization

After receiving the sealed envelope containing the Certificate of No Criminal Record from the local public security authority, applicants must return it to the notary office.

The notary office will proceed with the issuance of a Notarial Certificate of No Criminal Record.

The final notarial document includes an English translation or another language translation as requested by the applicant.

Processing Time: Approximately 3–5 days.

Additional Authentication for Overseas Use

Depending on the destination country, the notarized document may also need:

  • Apostille: Issued by China's Ministry of Foreign Affairs or an authorized office.
  • Consular Legalization: Authentication by the relevant embassy when applicable.

The required authentication procedure depends on the destination country's document requirements.

5. Application Authority

Certificate of No Criminal Record

Applications are handled by the district offices of the Exit and Entry Administration of the Tianjin Public Security Bureau.

The appropriate district office is determined by the applicant's residence in Tianjin.

Application Authority: District offices of the Exit and Entry Administration, Tianjin Public Security Bureau.

Applicants should contact the relevant district office to confirm its application arrangements and contact information.

The Tianjin Public Security Bureau publishes contact information for the district offices.

Notarization

The initial Request for Foreigner Criminal Record Check letter and the final Notarial Certificate of No Criminal Record are both handled by a local notary office in Tianjin.

Recommended Option: Tianjin Beifang Notary Public Office.

There are multiple notary offices in Tianjin. Applicants may contact a suitable office to confirm its current foreign-related notarization services and document requirements.

6. Special Requirements

The Notary Letter Must Be Obtained Before Applying to the Police

An important feature of the Tianjin application procedure is that applicants must first obtain a Request for Foreigner Criminal Record Check letter from a local notary office.

This letter is required for the subsequent application to the district Exit and Entry Administration office.

The notary office places the letter in a sealed envelope, which the applicant must deliver to the relevant public security authority.

Applying Through the Correct District Office

The district Exit and Entry Administration office responsible for the applicant's residence handles the application.

Applicants should contact the relevant district office before visiting to confirm the correct application location.

Temporary Residence Registration Forms

Foreign nationals applying for a Tianjin Certificate of No Criminal Record must have Temporary Residence Registration Forms issued by local police stations in Tianjin.

These documents help establish the applicant's residence registration history.

The Certificate Is Issued in a Sealed Envelope

After the public security authority completes the criminal record check, the Certificate of No Criminal Record is also placed in a sealed envelope.

Applicants must return this sealed envelope to the notary office for the final notarization procedure.

The envelope should remain sealed until it is delivered to the notary office.

Final Notarization and Translation

The notary office uses the certificate received in the sealed envelope to issue a Notarial Certificate of No Criminal Record.

The final notarial document includes an English translation or another language translation as requested by the applicant.

Sample Notarial Certificate of No Criminal Record

Notary offices across China issue Notarial Certificates of No Criminal Record in a consistent format, according to the information provided for this guide.

7. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Why do I need to visit a notary office before applying to the police in Tianjin?

Tianjin has a three-step application procedure. Applicants must first obtain a Request for Foreigner Criminal Record Check letter from a local notary office. This letter is required for the subsequent application to the district Exit and Entry Administration office.

What are the eligibility requirements for obtaining a Tianjin Certificate of No Criminal Record?

Applicants generally need to have Temporary Residence Registration Forms issued by local police stations in Tianjin and must have lived in Tianjin for at least 180 days.

Where should I apply for the Certificate of No Criminal Record?

You should apply at the district Exit and Entry Administration office of the Tianjin Public Security Bureau that has jurisdiction over your residence.

What documents are required?

Applicants generally need their passports, Temporary Residence Registration Forms, and the sealed Request for Foreigner Criminal Record Check letter issued by the notary office.

A Power of Attorney is also required where a third party is appointed.

How long does the entire procedure take?

According to the information provided for this guide:

  • Obtaining the notary letter: Approximately 1–3 working days.
  • Police application: Approximately 3 business days.
  • Final notarization: Approximately 3–5 days.

The overall processing time depends on the handling arrangements of the relevant authorities and notary office.

What happens after the police issue the certificate?

The Certificate of No Criminal Record is placed in a sealed envelope. Applicants must return the sealed envelope to the notary office for final notarization and translation.

Can I open the sealed envelope containing my certificate?

The sealed envelope should remain unopened and be delivered to the notary office for the final notarization procedure.

Can I request an English translation?

Yes. The final Notarial Certificate of No Criminal Record can include an English translation or another language translation as requested by the applicant.

Does the notarized certificate need an apostille?

This depends on the destination country. Some countries require an apostille, while others may require consular legalization.

Applicants should confirm the requirements with the receiving authority before proceeding.
